\section{What we are doing --- two things at once}

\textbf{Track A --- build the method.} An automated loop that searches for biological
\emph{mechanisms} rather than parameter values. It proposes a change to the model's structure
(``remove the pushing force'', ``add oriented cell division''), writes down what it expects
\emph{before} running, runs the simulation, measures, and records whether it was right. The
discipline that makes it worth building: a change of numbers can never masquerade as a new idea.

\textbf{Track B --- reproduce Okuda's result, as the proof that Track A works.} Okuda et al.\
(2018) grew a hollow ball of cells that spontaneously forms tubes, undulations and branches,
driven by a chemical pattern on its own surface. We are rebuilding that here. If the loop finds
the mechanism largely by itself, the method is demonstrated. \emph{The figure is not a side quest
--- it is the evidence.}

The two tracks feed each other: the reproduction is the honest test of the method, and the method
is what makes the reproduction more than hand-tuning.

\section{What we believe about the biology}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=grey!6,colframe=grey!45,boxrule=0.7pt,arc=2pt]
\begin{center}\large\textbf{Nothing \emph{measured}. But we now write down what we assume.}\end{center}

The register of \emph{results} is still empty, and should stay empty until a batch has been taken
with instruments that work. Every number we have was measured with at least one broken ruler in the
path.

What changed on 31 July is a different thing: the register of \textbf{assumptions} is no longer
empty. Eleven basics about cell tissue are written down in \texttt{discovery\_okuda/PREMISES.md} --- not a
literature review, but the minimum a person needs in order to look at one of our runs and say
``that cannot be right''. Each is phrased so that a computer can check it against a run, and each
one now does (Phase~1).

The distinction matters. A result is something we found out. A premise is something we are
\emph{assuming}, stated plainly enough that it can be caught being wrong. Writing the second kind
down is not a claim about biology --- it is a way of making our own mistakes visible.
\end{tcolorbox}

\section{The team of agents}\label{sec:agents}

\textbf{\code{ROLES.md} is the source of truth, and the code is checked against it in both
directions} by \code{roles.py -{}-check} --- the same discipline \code{PREMISES.md} has for the
biology. The roster had reached sixteen roles nobody could account for, because it lived in four
places at once: the code, a table in this note, a figure drawn by hand, and everybody's memory.
Four descriptions of one thing are three descriptions that are wrong. There is now one, and this
note does not repeat it.

\textbf{The roster was judged by a ledger, not by argument.} A live round spent 23.5 minutes across
sixteen calls, of which the Proposer took 14.8 --- 63\% of all agent time --- while the Judge and
the Referee were \emph{never called at all}. That is not a claim that they were redundant; it is a
record showing they had nothing to do. The cause was a borrowed design: \emph{Co-Scientist} runs a
tournament of hypotheses because it performs no experiments, so its proposals can only be debated.
We measure. Where a tournament would rank two ideas by argument, we run both.

\textbf{Three acts, and the division that matters.} Act~1 proposes, Act~2 measures, Act~3 decides.
Within that, the load-bearing split is between what is \emph{arithmetic} and what is
\emph{judgement}: the Critic decides whether a batch \textbf{can} be run and its refusals are
enumerable and arguable-with-never; peer-review decides whether it is \textbf{worth} running, which
is a judgement, and it advises rather than vetoes. A judgement that can silently cost twelve
GPU-hours is a veto wearing an adviser's name.

\textbf{A round that produces no evidence is aborted} and routes back to Act~1 carrying the
refusals. It is recorded in full but does not increment the round counter and enters no coverage
denominator --- counting it as a normal round is exactly how a log came to tell the Proposer
\code{coverage 0\%}, from which it correctly concluded its own ledger was broken. Two consecutive
aborts stop the campaign and ask, because a second abort means the refusal reasons were not
actionable, which is a fact about us rather than about the search.

\textbf{As of Phase~8 the loop can be run with no agent in it at all} (\S8.1), and every hand-off
between these roles is declared in \code{WIRING.md} and checked (\S8.2). Those two are what make
the paragraphs above testable rather than remembered.

\section{The phases}

Eight stages. \textbf{I stop at the end of each one and wait for you.} That is the partition.

\begin{phasebox}{Phase 0 --- Pre-flight: fix the instruments \hfill \dn\ (28 of 28)}
\textbf{Goal.} Make every measurement trustworthy. Nothing runs unattended until this lands. Not one
of the twenty-eight items is science; each one makes a question \emph{askable}.

\textbf{The last two, closed.} \emph{Comparing a batch at a common frame} --- the per-run evidence
horizon was right and did not make a BATCH comparable. If one run tears at frame 300 and another
holds to 700, comparing their ``final'' states puts a torn mesh beside a healthy one and reports
the difference as biology: both operands valid, the comparison wrong, which is the shape of every
error that has cost this campaign a batch. A batch is now read at one frame, the earliest horizon
in it, and the tool reports what that costs --- how much of each run is discarded, and a warning

references over in one step leaves a window where a key is written by half the code and read by the
other half, and since an absent metric reads as ``not measured'', the bug would surface as a round
that quietly learns nothing.

\begin{phasebox}{Phase 1 --- Teach the loop what a tissue is: the premise gate \hfill \dn}
\textbf{Why this became its own phase.} Ten defects were found in one day, every one by Cedric
looking at a picture, and all of the same kind: an operator or an instrument not doing what its name
says. The loop checked that runs finished, that the mesh was valid, that the numbers were finite. It
never once checked that the thing it had simulated was a tissue.

\textbf{Eleven basics, each one something a computer can run and fail}, in three tiers by cost: read
the recipe (instant, refuses a hopeless run before a GPU is touched); read the run (free, goes
loudly into the record); and run a small experiment of its own --- switch everything off and let the
ball sit for forty frames; it must not change size.

\textbf{A broken premise must be declared, not silenced.} Switching off growth to ask what a
protrusion is made of genuinely breaks premise~1, and that is science. So a recipe may waive a
premise \emph{in writing, with a reason}; an undeclared violation is a hard stop. That is the only
thing separating ``we tested an idea'' from ``the settings were wrong''.

\textbf{Every check was proved to fail before being trusted.} The one to look at: run against the
code as it stood that morning, a resting ball collapses \textbf{5.00 $\to$ 0.69} in forty frames.
That single check, which costs about a minute, would have caught the worst defect of the campaign on
day one.
\end{phasebox}

\begin{phasebox}{Phase 3 --- Switch on the agents that never run \hfill \dn\ (3 of 3)}
\textbf{Ablation is compulsory.} A causal claim needs two experiments: adding a mechanism and
seeing the shape appear shows it is \emph{sufficient}; taking it away and seeing the shape die
shows it is \emph{necessary}. Either alone is an opinion. Every claim now declares its kind and the
checker throws out a batch that asks for more than it tests, \emph{before} any simulation starts.
Sufficient earns ``is sufficient for''; both earn ``causes''; neither earns ``is associated with''.
\textbf{The word can no longer outrun the experiment.}

\textbf{The Grounder is wired.} The only agent that reads the paper had no call site anywhere ---
every entry point was exercised by its own smoke test alone. Batch construction now asks it, and it
answers with Okuda's numbers carrying the sentence they came from. They arrive as \emph{parameters},
so composition identity is unchanged: setting the starting size is a retune, not a new mechanism.
It \emph{advises} --- the faithful share of a batch inherits his numbers, the exploratory share is
free to leave them, because a search that may only stand where the paper stands cannot discover
that the paper is not the only place to stand.
\end{phasebox}

\begin{phasebox}{Phase 4 --- Measurements that can tell his regimes apart \hfill \dn}
\textbf{The classifier.} Sphere, undulation, tube, branched, plus \textbf{invalid} (the surface
passes through itself, so no shape reading means anything) and \textbf{unclear}, returned
deliberately near a boundary. A classifier that always answers can never be wrong, and one that can
never be wrong teaches nothing. ``Invalid'' beats every morphology --- this campaign has already
once reported a crumpled shell as a result. Until 1~August nothing called it: built, certified,
never asked.

\textbf{The calibration, and the surprise.} The plan was to tune until Okuda's handful of spots
appeared on a 2000-cell ball, then freeze. Counting spots over time --- for the first time ---
showed the pattern never settles: 104 spots at step~0, 19 at 100, 7 at 200, and \textbf{1 by step
400}, where it stays. It keeps merging. So ``the right number of spots'' is not a setting to be
found but \textbf{a moment to be chosen}, and the choice has to be stated rather than tuned into.
\end{phasebox}

\begin{phasebox}{Phase 4b --- Can the chemistry be simulated at all? \hfill \dn}
\textbf{Why this became a phase mid-flight.} The pre-launch stopped on a run whose chemistry turned
to infinities within a hundred frames. The rule that exists to prevent exactly this had been
\textbf{fifty times too generous} for as long as the campaign has existed.

\textbf{How.} The stability condition was written down as a \emph{comment}, worked out once, for the
settings of the day. Then the defaults changed, and Phase~2 deliberately widened the ranges so
Okuda's own values would be reachable --- and the arithmetic in the comment quietly stopped
describing the code. On top of that it counted one factor twice, reporting a fiftieth of the true
figure. \textbf{A comment cannot be re-derived; it can only be re-read, and nobody had cause to
re-read it.} The condition is now computed from the settings actually in force, and a composition
that breaches it is refused before it reaches a GPU rather than after.
\end{phasebox}

the pre-launch used one spot or none.

\textbf{And the three seeds were bit-identical} --- 0.501, 0.501, 0.501. \code{general.seed} was

described the floating-point reproducibility of a single trajectory.

draw the numbers that run drew. Departing from the convention would have made every reproduction
check compare two different experiments. A spec that declares a seed no operator consumes is now
refused outright.

\begin{phasebox}{Phase 5 --- The first live run: the agents do not talk to each other \hfill \prog}
\textbf{It ran, and that is the point.} Two rounds, eight simulations, 23 agent calls, 29
minutes of agent time. \textbf{One usable run. Map coverage 0\%.} Nothing was learned about the
biology --- and the reason turns out to be worth more than the biology would have been.

\textbf{What the machine got right, unprompted.} The Critic refused every bad run and named the
reason. The straggler killer cut two runs at 46 minutes against an 11-minute batch median. The
Supervisor diagnosed its own sterility --- \emph{``surprise 0.00: the batch is confirming what we
already believe, near-zero information. Push adversarial''} --- and a round with nothing
admissible said so instead of scoring what it had.

Every one of those is a correct judgement. \textbf{Not one of them reached another agent.}

\subsection*{The diagnosis: written by somebody, read by nobody}

The Proposer's own reasoning for round 4, verbatim, is the whole case:

\begin{quote}\small
``Header shows round 3, 0 runs, coverage 0\% \ldots another \textbf{counter-reset artifact}; real
record = memory.md.''\\[2pt]
``Those two batches proposed ALL FOUR valid single-op edits on parent 2, so parent 2 is
\textbf{fully PROPOSED} and \ldots cannot be built on further.''
\end{quote}

It was handed a summary built only from the lever map, and the lever map is filled \emph{after}
the Critic --- so a refused run contributes nothing to it. Eight simulations had died of diverged
chemistry and saturated buffers, and the Proposer was told ``0 runs, coverage 0\%''.

It then drew the only sane conclusion available from an insane input: that the \emph{ledger} was
broken, and its own prose memory was the real record. And it counted a family as explored because
it had \textbf{proposed} the edits, not because it had \textbf{measured} anything --- so it moved
on to a fresh parent believing the previous one was mapped, when nothing had been learned there
at all.

And the instruction the Proposer works from names three admitted metrics, of which the
\code{ta\_*} family is \textbf{absent from half the runs on disk} --- so it writes predictions
that cannot be scored, which resolve \emph{inconclusive} and drop out of the surprise rate
entirely. The loop is measuring its own information rate with a ruler that discards the
measurements.

\subsection*{The reason, stated once}

The campaign was built as a set of roles, each with a job, each doing it. What was never built is
the \emph{wiring}. Every agent writes; almost nothing reads. The result is not a loop but a fan:
work flows outward from the Proposer and never returns, so the system cannot be corrected by its
own findings. \textbf{A loop that is told only about its successes cannot avoid repeating its
failures} --- and this one had no successes to be told about.

That also explains the day's other defects, which looked unrelated and are not: a refusal printed
among hundreds of lines, an evidence horizon silently absent, a buffer flag raised after the
compute was spent, a state file grown past reading, a black movie. Each is a correct output with
no recipient.

\textbf{Drawing it is what made the shape obvious.} Every agent's output was plotted against
whoever read it, and the picture was a fan rather than a loop: work flowed outward from the
Proposer and almost nothing came back. Fourteen roles, and the only thing they shared was the
direction of travel. That picture is what turned the repair from ``wire these hand-offs'' into
``why are there so many hand-offs to wire'' --- which is \S\ref{sec:agents}, and Phase~6.

\subsection*{Fixed}

\textbf{Refusals are now evidence.} The Proposer receives what ran, what was refused and the
reason code, with the distinction stated plainly: an empty map beside a non-zero attempt count is
not a reset counter --- it means the compositions proposed so far \emph{cannot be simulated}, a
parent is explored when its edits produced evidence and never when they were merely proposed, and
the refusal codes say what to change.

\textbf{The black movie.} The colour scale is computed once for the whole run --- correctly, since
a per-frame rescale makes a decaying pattern and a stable one look identical --- but over samples
including non-finite values, and one of those makes both bounds non-finite. \textbf{Frame 0 was
perfectly good and was drawn black because frame 800 diverged.} The artefact reported ``nothing
happened'' for a run whose first half was fine. Now: finite values only, cells that left the model
drawn grey rather than white, and the count said out loud.

\textbf{The Biologist now speaks to somebody.} It had been running on every run since it was
written, and its verdict went to a terminal and to a field of \code{diag.json} that no prompt has
ever mentioned. On round 2 it broke five premises on the very run whose movie came out black ---
\emph{the activator had decayed to NaN, the chemistry was extinct} --- and a Reader read that
run's numbers and named a phenotype. Two hand-offs now exist: the per-run verdict goes to the
\textbf{Reader} that is about to read it, in the document's own words and with a field it must
fill in saying whether the specimen was sound; and the round-level tally goes to the
\textbf{Meta-review}, whose whole job is the recurring pattern. Across round 2 that tally reads
\emph{``P4 broken in 2 of 3 runs''} --- an edit family that cannot hold chemistry, which is a thing
to stop proposing rather than a thing to keep measuring.

\textbf{And the premises are read from \code{PREMISES.md}}, not retyped beside it. Two documents
saying the same thing in different words will disagree, and the one that drifts is always the prose
--- it is the one nothing executes. The titles, grades and stated checks now come from the
document, and the drift is reported in both directions. It found two immediately: premise~10
(neighbour exchange is rare) is written down and \textbf{nothing checks it}, and \code{P12} is
checked on every run and \textbf{appears nowhere in the document}.

\textbf{The two written records have a shape}, checked rather than suggested: an append-only log
with per-round fields, beside a state document of seven named sections. \code{memory.md} had
reached 1904 words across six appended blocks in a file whose purpose is to hold state.

\subsection*{What the diagnosis led to}

Wiring the hand-offs one at a time was the wrong repair, and stopping to ask \emph{why there were
so many to wire} was the right one. The answer is in \S\ref{sec:agents}: sixteen roles copied from
a system that runs no experiments. \textbf{The roster was rebuilt --- thirteen roles, three acts,
Judge and Referee dropped, the Collector made code} --- and the design is written down in
\code{ROLES.md} so that this decision cannot evaporate the way the Biologist's verdicts did.

\end{phasebox}

\begin{phasebox}{Phase 6 --- The rebuilt loop, and its first launch \hfill \done}
\textbf{Goal.} Rebuild the roster around measurement rather than debate, and launch it.

\subsection*{What the cold start found, and what it cost to misread it}
Across all 63 finished runs, \code{n\_protruding} was zero everywhere. \textbf{This project has
never produced a protrusion.} The loop reported that correctly --- and then gave up, which was the
expensive mistake, and it was ours. \textbf{Track~A asks a different question}: a composition that
patterns without protruding is a \emph{point on the map}, not a failure. On the corrected ranking,
\textbf{46 of 63 runs serve Track~A}. Only Track~B can run out of things worth running; the
operator landscape always has more of itself to understand.

\subsection*{Closing Phase 6 --- what was run}
Nine rounds, 2~August 2026. \textbf{79 hypotheses posed, 58 admitted as evidence, 21 refused.} Ten
defects found and fixed while it ran --- among them: the reading loop recorded \emph{one} run per
round and that run was a chimera, assembled from different runs' fields; and the ranking read
\code{premises\_broken} from the wrong dictionary, so it never once saw a broken premise.

\subsection*{Results, and what we conclude}
\textbf{The machinery works.} 30 predictions were refuted and 23 confirmed --- a surprise rate that
means the predictions were real rather than safe. \textbf{Tubes appeared, on sound specimens.}

\textbf{But the ceiling was set in round 3 and never moved.} No valid specimen exceeded
\code{protr\_peak} 1.295 or two tubes; every run above two tubes was invalid --- dead chemistry or a
self-intersecting sheet. And the valid frontier contains \textbf{no reaction--diffusion at all}:
\code{cell\_rd\_seed} and \code{morphogen\_growth\_3d}, but neither \code{cell\_diffuse} nor
\code{cell\_react}. The shape the campaign can make is not yet the shape the paper is about.

\subsection*{The main conclusion: the logic did not emerge}
\textbf{It was not in the agentic loop, and it was never going to arrive by itself.} Nine rounds of
capable agents produced conclusions no evidence supported --- ``the ceiling is FUNDAMENTAL'',
``division is NECESSARY'' --- because nothing in the loop could tell an untested possibility from a
refuted one, and \emph{could be} had nowhere to live. A campaign that cannot represent ``not yet
tried'' will write ``cannot be'' instead. That is what Phase~7 exists to fix, and it is a
\emph{structural} fix: the unearned conclusion has to be unwritable, not discouraged.
\end{phasebox}

\begin{phasebox}{Phase 7 --- The agentic loop update: giving the loop a logic \hfill \done}
\textbf{Goal.} Supply the reasoning discipline that Phase~6 proved will not emerge on its own ---
as \emph{structure and code}, not as another voice asking the others to be careful. No new agent.

\subsection*{Why this is one phase and not two}
The two defects have one cure. \emph{Absence of evidence read as evidence of absence} and
\emph{absence of an instrument read as absence of an effect} are the same sentence with a different
subject, and both are fixed by the same move: make the unearned conclusion \textbf{unwritable}, and
give the loop somewhere to put what it has not yet tried. Splitting them would build the machinery
twice.

\subsection*{1. \code{LOGIC.md}, and the checker that makes it real}
A third source-of-truth document, beside the two that already hold. \code{ROLES.md} stays honest
because \code{roles.py --check} compares it against the code \emph{in both directions};
\code{PREMISES.md} stays honest because the Biologist mechanically parses it. \code{LOGIC.md}
states what may be concluded from what, and \code{logic.py --check} \textbf{refuses a malformed
claim at the moment the Meta-review writes it} --- so a bad claim never enters the document the
Proposer reads. A rule agents are merely \emph{asked} to honour is the failure mode being removed.

\subsection*{2. Four modalities, and a home for \emph{could be}}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llp{6.2cm}@{}}
\textbf{can be} & demonstrated positive & one instance suffices \\
\textbf{cannot be} & universal negative & must carry its quantifier \\
\textbf{cannot not be} & necessity & the most expensive claim of all \\
\textbf{could be} & untested, open & \textbf{free --- and today unrepresentable} \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\code{memory.md} has exactly two buckets, so untested territory collapses into \emph{impossible}.
That is how \emph{we never tried reaction-diffusion} became \emph{the only remaining route is a
different geometry}. The third bucket is mineable by the Proposer and may never be cited as a
negative.

\subsection*{3. Every negative carries its conditions}
Four of seven positive claims carry a falsifier because the template has a \code{Falsifiable by:}
field; two of nine prohibitions carry any escape because it has no field for them. So: a trap,
an \textsc{inert}, a \textsc{necessary}, a \emph{closed} or an \emph{exhausted} records the runs
supporting it, the conditions held fixed, and what would refute it --- or it does not enter.
Necessity is held to the highest bar, because it quantifies over everything not removed. Claims
formed before a known apparatus fix are marked \emph{provisional} and queue first for retest.

\subsection*{3b. Converging observations, and what an ablation can carry}
A negative needs \emph{converging} support, not repeated support. Rounds~4--8 produced
bit-identical results under four different composition hashes, and the morphogen-closure claim
cites \emph{ten or more runs} --- every one of them Gierer--Meinhardt \textbf{with} division, while
the one run without it was stable and never counted. Ten runs, \textbf{one} observation. A rule
demanding ``three supporting runs'' would have passed the worst claim in the file.

So support is counted in \textbf{distinct condition-signatures}, computed from the cited runs'
\code{composition.json} on disk: strip the claim's subject, and whatever remains identical across
every supporting run is an unvaried confounder. Two consequences come free --- the
\code{conditions:} field is \emph{computed rather than authored}, so it cannot be satisfied with
prose; and a confounded set is visible at write time instead of five rounds later.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lp{8.4cm}@{}}
\textbf{can be} & one observation --- existence is cheap \\
\textbf{could be} & none --- the default for untried territory \\
\textbf{cannot be} & three independent signatures, conditions computed \\
\textbf{cannot not be} & three independent \emph{plus} one deliberate counterexample attempt
that failed \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\textbf{The ablation is the right instrument, read the wrong way.} The loop was never short of
ablations --- \code{remove\_op divide\_3d0} is one, and it ran twice, six rounds apart, with
opposite results. The asymmetry is that the \emph{same} experiment supports a positive strongly
and a negative weakly: \emph{removing X killed the effect} says X matters \emph{here};
\emph{removing X changed nothing} is offered as X is inert \emph{everywhere}, which is pure
absence of evidence. Six \textsc{inert} claims rest on a single null ablation each --- the reading
that most needs corroboration got the least. Hence: a \textbf{null} ablation must be repeated
across independent backgrounds before it may be written as a negative, while a \textbf{positive}
ablation still passes on one.

And an ablation is \emph{not} a counterexample attempt. Ablation removes X from a working recipe;
a counterexample asks whether the effect is reachable \emph{at all} without X, and may change other
operators to compensate. Necessity requires the second, which is why \emph{``division is
necessary''} survived six rounds --- nobody ever tried to build a bud without it. When round~8
finally did, by ablating on a different parent, it produced a clean bud with a fission neck.

\textbf{Under-supported claims are demoted, never deleted.} One null ablation is real evidence; it
is simply not a universal negative. It becomes \code{could be inert (n=1, conditions: ...)} ---
minable by Track~A, citable by nobody as a prohibition. The revisit queue is then literally
\emph{the list of claims sitting below their support bar}, and the checker's default action is
\emph{demote}, which costs nothing and self-corrects, rather than \emph{reject}, which stalls a
round.

\subsection*{4. The tracks become asymmetric}
\textbf{Track A opens.} It may violate any trap, and must spend a budgeted quota of slots per round
\emph{revisiting} claims --- preferring single-run claims and provisional ones. A revisit must vary
a \textbf{named confounder}, which is what would have caught a family closed on runs that all
shared an unvaried one, and what stops the bit-identical duplicates of rounds~4--8.
\textbf{Track B constrains.} It cites a named Okuda morphology and settings, respects traps, and
may not breed from an invalid specimen.

The Proposer's ablations therefore gain two duties rather than a quota: repeat a \textbf{null}
ablation on an independent background before it may harden into a negative, and for any necessity
claim mount a \textbf{rescue attempt} --- permitted to change other operators to compensate ---
because that is the experiment necessity actually rests on.

\subsection*{5. No conclusion about an unmeasured property}
If a conclusion needs a quantity no admitted instrument reports, the correct output is a
\textbf{request for an instrument}, not a verdict. \emph{Sphere} may not be concluded from a run
whose pattern was never measured --- which is precisely what happened to the finest Turing pattern
in the campaign.

\subsection*{6. The Metrologist builds instruments --- on demand, not on schedule}
The Metrologist gains the power to propose, implement and certify a new instrument against known
answers, then admit it. It is \textbf{triggered, never scheduled}: the rule above files a request
into the \code{Backlog} that already exists (\code{escalation.py}, \code{operator\_requests.jsonl}),
and the Metrologist wakes only when the same gap has been requested repeatedly. It runs on its own
budget line, outside the round ceiling, because it is not per-round work --- expect it to fire
perhaps twice in twenty rounds. The cost amortises to nothing: certified once, the instrument is
code that runs free forever. The first request is already known: \textbf{wavelength, domain count
and contrast on the activator field} --- the variable that actually governs budding.

\subsection*{7. Paying for it: the ceiling stops binding}
Round~10 spent 33.4 minutes against a 25-minute ceiling and began dropping Interpreters. The
breakdown says where it goes: \textbf{interpreter 4 calls / 13.0 min (39\%) and reader 9 calls /
9.8 min (29\%)} --- two-thirds, both per-run. Cutting effort globally would blunt the roles doing
the reasoning, so instead:
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=1pt]
\item \textbf{Lower model effort on the description roles only.} The Reader and the Eye-check
\emph{label}; they do not infer. That is the 12.5 minutes that can be halved without touching a
judgement.
\item \textbf{Move the Interpreter earlier in the order.} It runs last, so it is the role
truncated --- the loop currently pays for the most expensive analysis and then discards it.
\end{itemize}
Those two alone bring the round under the ceiling, and they buy the headroom Track~A's revisit
quota will spend.

\subsection*{Exit criteria}
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=1pt]
\item \code{logic.py --check} reports zero malformed claims, and \textbf{rejects the nine-round
record} of Phase~6 --- every one of the eight errors must be caught by replaying the archived
\code{memory.md} through it.
\item A \emph{could be} entry exists, is mined by the Proposer, and is never cited as a negative.
\item A Track~A slot violates a trap, varies a named confounder, and the result is recorded.
\item The Metrologist certifies one new instrument, and a previously-null run is re-read with it.
\item A round completes inside the LLM ceiling with no role skipped.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{What you get.} A loop whose conclusions are as good as its measurements --- and which says
so when they are not.

\subsection*{Closing Phase 7}
\textbf{The phase turned out to be wiring, not construction.} Almost everything it needed already
existed and had never been called: \code{CLAIM\_KINDS}, the batch and memory checks, the reservoir
counters, the pattern-scale instrument --- already \emph{certified} --- the status setter, the
allowed-verb table. The external audit's closing line is the phase in one sentence:
\emph{``writing it to a file is not the same as handing it over.''}

\textbf{The ablation asymmetry}, which is Cedric's and is the sharpest idea in it: the same
experiment supports a positive strongly and a negative weakly. A null ablation must repeat on an
independent background; a positive passes on one. And necessity needs a \emph{rescue attempt} ---
a different experiment, not a re-reading of the same one.

\textbf{The false start of 3~August, and what it taught.} The ceiling cried wolf; the clean start
was not clean, and handed a fresh Proposer a memory saying \code{THIS BODY IS CLOSED}. The lesson
is the phase's own turned on itself: a rule that is written but not wired changes nothing, and that
applies to the rules \emph{about} rules. Which is why Phase~8 begins by making the loop runnable
with no agent in it at all --- the only way to find a broken hand-off without paying for a round.
\end{phasebox}

\begin{phasebox}{Phase 8 --- Entities, edges and sweeps: the loop stops being a script \hfill \done}
\textbf{Goal.} Three things the 3~August runs showed cannot be patched one at a time: give the loop
a \emph{declared structure} it can be checked against, close the audit findings that have been
open since the graph was drawn, and schedule the parameter sweep that has been implemented and
never run.

\subsection*{Why now, and why one phase}
Six defects were fixed on 3~August and \emph{not one of them was a logic error}:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lp{7.4cm}@{}}
\code{composition.json} & written by \code{write\_config}, never by recon: a producer with no
consumer \\
\code{run[:14]} & a display truncation read back as an identifier \\
\code{batch\_ok=None} & a verdict computed, never applied \\
menu preamble & a contract asserted in prose, enforced nowhere \\
\code{max\_per\_parent=6} & a limit set for one purpose binding another \\
\code{say()} vs the wrapper & two owners of one property, folding at 100 and at 96 \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent Every one is an \textbf{interface defect}: the components are individually correct and
disagree at the seam. That is the class of bug patching cannot retire, because each patch closes
one seam and the next round exposes the next. Sixteen more declared edges are unchecked today.

\subsection*{1. Settled by construction: the loop runs with no agent in it}
Every defect above was found by \emph{launching}, which is the most expensive instrument available:
a round costs ten agent-minutes and half an hour of cluster before it will tell you that a display
name was read as an identifier. \textbf{The loop must be testable without an agent and without a
cluster.} One seam --- \code{run\_agent} --- carries every model call, and one --- \code{cluster}
--- carries every job, so both can be substituted for deterministic stubs that return
schema-valid answers, including the \emph{wrong} answers we have actually seen: an edit that names
a phenotype, a verdict with no flag, a caption with no JSON, a proposal under the wrong key.

A full three-act round then runs offline, in seconds, for nothing, and the questions it answers are
exactly the ones that have cost us rounds: does every declared artifact get written, does every
gate fire, does the batch fill, is the record complete before its readers run. A regression becomes
a failing test rather than a wasted evening. \textbf{This is built first, because it is what makes
the rest of the phase checkable.}

\begin{resultbox}{8.1 --- Built, and what it found immediately \hfill \done}
\textbf{Three seams, not two.} \code{run\_claude} (not \code{run\_agent} --- \code{agents/} is on
\code{sys.path}, so \code{llm} and \code{agents.llm} are two module objects for one file, and
patching either alone leaves the other live: the first ``offline'' round spent \$0.37 while
printing that it was free), the cluster, and --- found only by running --- \textbf{the VLM}. A
23\,GB model load is not offline by any reading, and a harness that takes five minutes will not be
run before a launch, which is the only thing it is for.

\smallskip
\textbf{\code{offline.py}} --- a full three-act round in \textbf{$\approx$6 seconds}, no agent, no
GPU, no VLM. \textbf{\code{test\_offline.py}} --- \textbf{8 cases, 8 passing}, each one a round we
have already paid for: the prompt arrives whole; no path reaches the real CLI (proved with a
\code{subprocess.Popen} tripwire); a clean round fills its batch and reaches Act~3; phenotype edits
are refused and repaired \emph{in the same round}; a proposal under the wrong key is still read; a
reply with no JSON does not fabricate a batch; \code{REJECT} with no flag is still a rejection; a
truncated name resolves to its run.

\smallskip
\textbf{What it found on its first honest run --- the worst defect of the campaign.}
\code{run\_agent} contained \code{prompt.split("BREVITY")[0]}, and the Proposer's template pastes
\code{\{BREVITY\}} at character~331 of a 16{,}093-character prompt. \textbf{The agent received 331
characters}: no legal-move menu, no refusals, no history, no output schema. It invented
\code{add branching} and \code{add chemotaxis} because it had never been shown the operators --- and
when it reported \emph{``I lack the injected \textsc{legal moves} menu (this call omitted it)''},
that was \textbf{literally true} and was read here as a hallucination. Four rounds were spent on
the consequences, including the recon Proposer naming zero runs, whose JSON schema sat past
character~331 as well. Excising the \emph{block} rather than truncating at it: $331 \to 18{,}025$
characters, 36~moves, schema present.

\smallskip
\textbf{Two faults were the harness's own} --- worth recording, because inventing failures is the
characteristic way a fake goes wrong. Inferring the batch size with a loose regex matched
``1~slot'' inside a pasted refusal block, so it proposed a batch of one; and a hand-written metrics
series omitted \code{act\_mean}, so the Diagnostician read all six runs as diverged and stopped the
round over an apparatus fault that did not exist. Both now \emph{template from real files} instead
of being written by hand, which is the rule this box exists to state: \textbf{a fake that invents
failures trains you to ignore the alarm.}

\smallskip
\textbf{Also landed with it.} Same-round repair when the Critic guts a batch, because its refusals
are mechanical and have a right answer --- measured $1$ of $12 \to 12$ of $12$ on round~2's real
proposal, one extra call, bounded at one so it cannot loop. Attrition written as a number
(\code{asked/proposed/refused/delivered}) rather than two prints a reader must subtract.
\code{REVISE} reads as a rejection. \code{[trace]} off by default. And ``not checkable'' no longer
fires on recon slots, which carry no prediction \emph{by design} --- it was describing the design
as a defect, three lines per run, twelve runs a round.
\end{resultbox}

\subsection*{2. The entities, as classes and a registry}
\code{ROLES.md} already declares who exists and \code{roles.py --check} keeps it honest --- but it
checks \emph{existence only}. Each role also declares \textbf{Sends to:}, and nothing has ever
verified that those fifteen edges correspond to a read anywhere in the code. So a \code{Role} and
an \code{Artifact} become objects in a registry, each artifact naming its writer, its readers and
its schema, and the checker fails the round when:

\begin{itemize}\itemsep2pt
\item an artifact is written and never read --- five exist today: \code{causal\_descriptions.md},
      \code{diagnoses.jsonl}, \code{archivist.jsonl}, \code{holes.jsonl}, \code{logic\_report.jsonl}
\item a declared \textbf{Sends to:} edge has no corresponding read
\item a prompt asserts a guarantee no checker enforces
\item a rendering is assigned to an identifier field
\end{itemize}

\noindent This is the same idiom a fourth time --- a document that is the source of truth beside a
checker that enforces it --- applied to the one thing currently specified only in comments.

\begin{resultbox}{8.2 --- Built \hfill \done}
\code{WIRING.md} declares every artifact, its writer and its readers; \code{wiring.py} derives the
same graph from the code and reports the difference. \textbf{The rule that carries the weight needs
no analysis at all: a new artifact must be declared, \emph{with a reader}.} It is deterministic, it
is the rule that was broken five times, and it caught \code{batch\_attrition.jsonl} --- which I had
added an hour earlier and nothing read --- within the hour.

\smallskip
\textbf{The derivation is a corroborating heuristic, and says so.} Writes go through class
constructors (\code{BudgetLedger(path=...)}, \code{Register(path=REGISTER)}) and following those
statically needs real dataflow analysis. Two rounds of sharpening --- function-scoped windows, then
module-constant aliases --- took the false alarms from about twenty-five to none, because
\textbf{a checker that cries wolf is one nobody runs before a launch}. Where analysis cannot reach,
the declaration decides; that is exactly how \code{PREMISES.md} works.

\smallskip
\textbf{The suite is now 11 cases, 11 passing}, including one that exists only to make the checker
prove it bites: a row declared with no reader \emph{must} be objected to, or a green tick is
indistinguishable from a parser that matched no rows at all.
\end{resultbox}

\subsection*{3. The audit findings, closed}
The round record written after its own consumers; the Archivist's \code{roll\_back} truncating at
eleven characters against a twelve-character name; the Eye-check defaulting \code{supports=True},
so an unparsed caption reads as agreement; recon runs that cannot seed the frontier because a
verbatim copy carries no composition; and \code{\_dbg\_shrink.py}, which does not import.

\begin{resultbox}{8.3 --- Closed, and one that will not close \hfill \done}
\textbf{The eye no longer agrees by default.} \code{j.get("supports", True)} made an unparsed reply
--- a timeout, a caption the model never produced --- read as the Eye-check \emph{agreeing} with the
numbers. That is the one direction this role must never fail in: it is the only channel in the loop
that looks at \emph{shape} rather than at number, so a silent yes removes the only thing that could
contradict the arithmetic, and removes it invisibly. Three states now, and an unheard eye neither
supports nor blocks.

\smallskip
\textbf{The Archivist's identifier is no longer truncated.} \code{comp[:11]} fitted the column and
\emph{measurably} collides on the current log --- hashes run 12 to 28 characters --- and the
Archivist names a branch back to the Supervisor \emph{from that table}. A collision there rolls the
campaign back to the wrong composition. The region is display and may be cut; the hash is identity
and may not. Same disease as \code{run[:14]}.

\smallskip
\textbf{Recon runs can be rebuilt into parents} --- operators, implementations and parameters
recovered from the spec, honestly partial about the connections a spec does not carry.

\smallskip
\textbf{And the finding underneath it, which is not closed.} The rebuilt parents are still refused
by the Critic: \code{cell\_diffuse0.d\_h=2.0} against a declared ceiling of \code{0.346},
parameters an emitter never receives, \code{impl: default} for an operator that has no such
implementation. \textbf{The runs this project has actually made sit outside the space it is
searching.} \code{from\_preset} warns of precisely this in its own docstring --- \emph{``if a recipe
we already trust cannot be built from legal one-edit moves, the campaign is searching a space that
does not contain our own evidence''} --- and that validation gate is failing. Whether the ranges are
wrong or the runs are is \textbf{a decision, not a patch}; the loop now says so once per run and
falls back rather than pretending.

\smallskip
\textbf{The harness isolates each round.} Sharing the campaign's accumulating state made every
successive offline round harder to satisfy --- the fourth in a row had every edit refused as a
duplicate of its own predecessors. \emph{A test whose result depends on how many times it has been
run is not a test.} The real campaign is copied aside and restored at exit.
\end{resultbox}

\subsection*{4. The sweep that was built and never scheduled}
\code{--mode theta} exists, and its discipline is already mechanical: \code{comp\_hash}
\textbf{excludes} theta, so a retune cannot register as a new mechanism --- a change of numbers
cannot masquerade as a new idea because the identity function says so, not because an agent was
asked to be careful. What is missing is only wiring: \code{plan()} emits
\code{recon} and \code{composition} and never \code{theta}; nothing chooses the base composition,
the parameter or the values; and \code{build\_theta\_batch} sweeps \emph{one} parameter of
\emph{one} composition, so mixtures of parameters are not expressible at all. Track~A is not only
``does this mechanism matter'' but ``what does it do as you turn it up'', and the second question
has never been asked.

\begin{resultbox}{8.4 --- A parameter change is a move on the menu \hfill \done}
Cedric's design, and better than the separate mode it replaces: \code{set\_param} becomes an edit
kind beside \code{add\_op}, \code{remove\_op} and \code{set\_impl}, so the Proposer chooses
structural and parameter moves from \emph{one} list and can \textbf{mix them in a single batch} ---
instead of a whole round having to be one or the other.

\smallskip
\textbf{The discipline holds by construction, not by instruction.} \code{comp\_hash} already
excludes parameters, so a retune yields the \emph{same} composition identity and can never register
as a new mechanism. ``A change of numbers can never masquerade as a new idea'' is true because the
identity function says so --- asserted as a test, not asked of an agent.

\smallskip
Two points per parameter, taken from each operator's own \code{(lo, hi, default)}, so a sweep cannot
leave the space the Critic admits. \textbf{The specimen is not an axis}: substrate parameters,
\code{n\_cells} and \code{seed} are excluded, because \code{round.py} grounds starting conditions
for a reason it learned when two slots ran 500 cells against a control at 2000 --- any difference
between them confounded the edit with a fourfold change in specimen size.

\smallskip

\section*{Appendix --- words I could not avoid}

Only these four. Everything else above is ordinary English.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Cell sheet (or mesh)} --- the model of the tissue: a hollow ball of polygonal cells
sharing walls, like a football.
\item \textbf{Operator} --- one mechanism as a reusable building block: ``cells divide'',
``chemical spreads'', ``surface pulls tight''. A model is a combination of these.
\item \textbf{The loop} --- the automated cycle: propose a change, predict the outcome, run,
measure, record whether the prediction held.
\item \textbf{Phase} --- one of the eight stages above. Our agreed stopping points.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{The four measurements, renamed}

Agreed 31 July. The old names misled in three separate ways and are being retired: \code{protr}
reads as a length but is a \emph{ratio}; \code{final} became ambiguous the moment we introduced
the evidence horizon; and \code{Q} said nothing at all. The rename happens as one mechanical pass
once the current work lands, with old records aliased on read --- the archive is never rewritten.

\toprule
\textbf{was} & \textbf{is} & \textbf{means}\\

\code{protr} & \code{elongation}
