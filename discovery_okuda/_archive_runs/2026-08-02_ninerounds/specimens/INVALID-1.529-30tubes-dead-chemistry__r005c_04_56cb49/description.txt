DESCRIPTION:
The simulation begins with a spherical vesicle composed of a dense, uniform mesh of small cells. Over time, the cells undergo a process of growth and division, causing the overall structure to expand and become more irregular in shape. The internal organization of the cells shifts from a tight, uniform packing to a more complex, faceted arrangement of larger polygonal units. By the final frames, the vesicle has significantly increased in volume and exhibits a jagged, crystalline-like surface geometry.

The primary object is a large, spherical vesicle composed of numerous interconnected cells. These cells are initially small and white, but they evolve into larger, irregularly shaped polygonal units.
