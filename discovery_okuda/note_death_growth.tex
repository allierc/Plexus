\textbf{Scope.} This note is about one addition to the framework: a second reaction--diffusion
species, and the two operators that consume it --- growth inhibition and cell death. It asks whether
that addition is \emph{sound}, on three axes: as biology, as code, and as Plexus2. \textbf{It does
not ask whether the mechanism achieves the campaign's goal.} Twenty-four preliminary runs exist in
\code{log/okuda} and they raised a specific set of defects; \S6 is that list, structured, with each
one's status. A mechanism whose implementation is unverified cannot be compared with an observation
at all, so the goal question is deferred until this note's gates close.

\section*{1.\; The mechanism}

\textbf{One field cannot pattern two processes.} With a single Gray--Scott pair every operator reads
the same activator --- growth is gated on it, death is gated on it, the purse-string selects on it ---
so the tissue carries exactly one map. ``Where it grows'' and ``where it dies'' are then the same
region or exact complements of it, by construction, and no setting of any parameter separates them.
This is a property of the representation.

Two species is two maps. Biologically this is unremarkable and well attested: a tissue carries many
morphogens at once, on different length scales, read by different targets. Two are enough to state
the smallest interesting case --- one field says \emph{grow here}, another says \emph{stop} or
\emph{die here} --- and that is the case this note verifies.

\textbf{The two consumers are drawn from different literatures.} Growth inhibition is lateral
inhibition in the Gierer--Meinhardt sense: a diffusible signal that suppresses the response of the
cells around a source. Patterned death is Monier \emph{et al.} (2015), where apoptotic force drives
epithelial folding in \emph{Drosophila} --- and it is the only \emph{inward} deformation available
in this vocabulary, every other operator pushing the sheet outward.

\textbf{The arithmetic that fixes the representation.} A dying cell leaves an epithelium by losing
neighbours until it is a triangle; a triangle collapses to a point as a T2 transition, $V-2$, $E-3$,
$F-1$, so $\chi = V-E+F$ is unchanged and the sheet stays closed at genus~0. Collapsing a face with
$k>3$ sides would merge $k$ vertices into one and leave a vertex of degree $k$, \textbf{which a
trivalent sheet cannot represent}. So death cannot be one operator: it is mark, then shed, then
extrude, and the shedding belongs to \op{reconnect\_t1\_3d}. That is not a hidden coupling; it is
the mechanism. A cell leaves an epithelium by losing neighbours.

Two ratios fix the proportions. The species' wavelengths must differ or the second map is
redundant: measured on a static sphere, species~A at $\chi=1.3$ holds $2$ spots at a spacing of
$6.99$ cell diameters and species~B at $\chi=0.4$ holds $15$ at $3.18$ --- a factor of $2.2$, with
\textbf{A} the coarse map. $\chi$ sets this, not the diffusivity pair: at $\chi=1.3$ with doubled
diffusivities the field does not pattern at all. And the clearing time of a dying cell is fixed by
two numbers and nothing else (Eq.~\eqref{eq:clear}).

\section*{2.\; Equations and symbols}

\textbf{Two independent systems in one buffer.} \code{chem} is width~4; species~A occupies columns
$(0,1)$ and species~B columns $(2,3)$. Each has its own seeder, diffusion and reaction, and each
operator declares which pair it owns through \code{chan}:
%
\begin{equation}
\frac{\partial a_X}{\partial t}=D^X_a\nabla^2 a_X + f(a_X,u_X),\qquad
\frac{\partial u_X}{\partial t}=D^X_u\nabla^2 u_X + g(a_X,u_X),\qquad X\in\{A,B\},
\label{eq:two}
\end{equation}
%
with $f,g$ Gray--Scott. The two systems are coupled \emph{only} through the operators that read
them, never through the buffer --- which is a property that must be gated (G7), not assumed.

\textbf{Growth, and the zero it did not have.} The standing law is purely activating,
%
\begin{equation}
\dot v_{\rm eq}=\texttt{rate}\cdot\bigl(\rho+\mathrm{hill}(a_A)\bigr),\qquad
\mathrm{hill}(x)=\frac{x^{n}}{x_{\rm sw}^{n}+x^{n}}\in[0,1],
\label{eq:growth}
\end{equation}
%
so the slowest a cell can grow is $\texttt{rate}\cdot\rho$, and $\rho>0$ is required by premise~P1
(a tissue that adds no material is not growing). The inhibitor supplies the missing zero,
multiplicatively:
%
\begin{equation}
\dot v_{\rm eq}=\texttt{rate}\cdot\bigl(\rho+\mathrm{hill}(a_A)\bigr)\cdot
\bigl(1-\mathrm{hill}_{\rm inh}(\hat a_B)\bigr),\qquad \hat a_B=a_B/\max_f a_B .
\label{eq:inhib}
\end{equation}
%
$\hat a_B$ is normalised to the inhibitor's own maximum for the same reason $a_{\rm sw}$ is a
fraction: a threshold stated absolutely, against a field whose scale the chemistry sets, is either
always on or always off.

\textbf{Death, as three steps and one closed-form time.}
%
\begin{equation}
V^0_f \leftarrow (1-s)V^0_f,\qquad \text{extrude when } V^0_f<c\,v_{\rm ref},\qquad
\boxed{\;\tau_{\rm clear}=\frac{\ln c}{\ln(1-s)}\ \text{ticks}\;}
\label{eq:clear}
\end{equation}

\textbf{Volume is conserved into the survivors, and the conservation is bounded.} When $f$ dies its
remaining material is shared over its live ring neighbours, and the increment is capped so the
recipient stays inside the integrator's basin:
%
\begin{equation}
c_g \leftarrow c_g + \min\!\Bigl(\frac{\text{amt}/|\mathcal L|}{V^0_g},\;
c_{\max}-c_g\Bigr),\qquad
\mathcal L=\{g\in\mathcal N(f): V^0_g\ge c\,v_{\rm ref}\},
\label{eq:conserve}
\end{equation}
%
with $c_{\max}$ the field's own running maximum. Material that will not fit is \emph{dropped and
counted} in \code{apop\_spill}, so the conservation law reports its own error rather than
violating itself silently. Both restrictions are gated (G4, G5).

\textbf{A death RATE, not a death set}: with $N$ live cells, $|\{\text{marked}\}|\le\phi N$, and when
the cap binds the worst candidates are taken by the mode's own severity. Geometric modes are exempt,
because a set chosen once has no flux.

\section*{3.\; The Plexus2 container}

No new set. What is new is a \emph{family} the vesicle lacked --- \emph{Die} --- and a second species
inside one state buffer. Three claims about the container have to hold, and each is a gate:

\begin{enumerate}\itemsep0pt
\item \textbf{Two instances of one operator are ADDITIVE, not overwriting.} \op{cell\_react}
  returns a delta over the whole \code{chem} tensor and writes zeros outside the pair its
  \code{chan} names. Without that, a second instance overwrites the first and ``two species'' is a
  name for one. (G7)
\item \textbf{\op{apoptosis\_3d} is \code{structural}, not \code{lateral}.} It changes how many
  entities exist, so it may not be a pure state map: it renumbers faces, and every per-face array
  and every \emph{pending delta} must be permuted with it. (G3)
\item \textbf{A named population is a seed window, one level up.} \op{list}, \op{band} and \op{cone}
  choose a population ONCE. Re-evaluated every frame, a death sentence becomes a death rate --- the
  same argument the paper makes for seeding, where an initial condition re-applied every step is a
  moving boundary condition. (G6)
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Schedule.} Death runs \emph{between growth and the mechanics}: growth sets the targets, death
overrides them for the sentenced, and the relaxation sees both in one tick, so a cell extruded this
frame has its hole closed this frame. Measured, same composition with death appended after the
recorder instead: grip $0.031$ against $0.110$. There is no \code{substep\_dt} block --- this vesicle
integrates at one rate and \op{shape\_energy\_3d} carries \code{relax\_iters} internally.

\textbf{Eleven bookkeeping, five closed form, three measurement --- and all three of the last are
blocked} for want of a \code{units:} block, the same defect the \code{ecm} study found when

cells.} Declaring units is the single change that would move three gates from blocked to failable.

\section*{5.\; What the runs establish}

Twenty-four exploratory runs on \op{r001\_03} and fifteen dedicated gate runs. Three results are
properties of the operators rather than of any morphology, and are the ones this note rests on.

\textbf{Position dominates rate.} Death patterned in SPACE deforms the body at a thirtieth of the
cell cost of death patterned by rate:

\op{sc\_waist} (an equatorial band)&  332 & 14486 & 1.174 & 0.328 & 0.807\\

Scattered death thins a ball into a smaller ball; a chosen population is a cut.

\textbf{The throughput lever is $\tau_{\rm clear}$, not the cap.} Fifty times $\phi$ moves the death
count by $1.7\times$; tripling $s$ at fixed $\phi$ moves it by $4.7\times$. The queue does not fill,
so what binds is how fast a sentenced cell reaches the extrusion threshold (G14).

\textbf{Inhibition is monotone and is a brake.} $12692 \to 6920 \to 4859 \to 4154$ cells as
\code{inhib\_sw} tightens, with \code{protr\_final} falling $1.095 \to 1.020$. Eq.~\eqref{eq:inhib}
asserts a multiplicative shutdown and the tissue size follows it; the shape does not sharpen.

\section*{7.\; What this licenses, and what it does not}

\textbf{Licensed.} Two RD species coexist in one buffer without leaking (G7), which is the structural
claim the whole addition rests on. The Die family is sound as bookkeeping: selectors fire where they
should and nowhere else (G1), the sheet stays closed (G2), a topology change carries its pending
deltas (G3), the count is exact (G11), the rate is a rate (G10), and a named population is a seed
window rather than a per-frame rule (G6). Growth can be switched off (G9), and the collapse is a
genuine T2 (G12).

\textbf{Not licensed.} \textbf{G5} is the one that matters most: until it closes, the operator can
drive the chemistry out of its basin and a run's numbers are then about an integrator.
\textbf{G13} means the clearing time --- the quantity every death experiment is denominated in ---
is asserted and not measured. \textbf{No gate is a measurement} (G17--G19 blocked for want of a
\code{units:} block), so nothing here has been compared with a cell.

\textbf{The mechanism should not enter the loop until G5, G8 and G13 close.} G5 because a mechanism
that can NaN the chemistry will produce runs the campaign records as evidence; G8 because an
operator that changes runs which do not use it corrupts every comparison in the ledger; G13 because
the clearing time is the quantity every death experiment is denominated in. G16 and I4/I5 are about
the experiments, not the operators, and can close afterwards.
