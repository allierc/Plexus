\section{Why a second repository, and why this one}

One repository cannot show saturation. The measurement \texttt{plexus2.tex} App.~E.1 asks for ---
\emph{does decomposing a broad collection of frameworks converge on a compact reusable
vocabulary?} --- is a claim about what happens \emph{across} frameworks. With $n=1$ the curve is a
shape, not an argument.

\textbf{Target: CompuCell3D (Cellular Potts).} Nominated by the jax-morph note before any of this
work, on the grounds that it is \emph{the biggest step away from everything we have}: a cell is not
a point with per-cell state, it is a \textbf{set of lattice sites}. Its position is a derived
centroid. Its dynamics are Metropolis flips of site ownership. There is no per-cell equation to
subtract.

That is the point. Every measurement in the jax-morph atlas rested on cells being points and a
differential test being a subtraction. \textbf{If the procedure is general it survives this; if it
is secretly a particle-framework procedure, this is where it breaks.}

The prediction --- made before running anything, and the reason this note exists --- is in
\code{TRANSFER.md}.

\section{The oracle --- where the risk actually is}

\code{TRANSFER.md} predicted the oracle would be the largest risk and a \emph{target} risk rather
than a \emph{procedure} risk. It is, and here is the state of it.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}l l p{8.2cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{gate} & \textbf{state} & \\
\midrule
installs & \dn & CompuCell3D 4.10.0 from its own conda channel into an isolated environment
(\code{/workspace/.conda\_envs/cc3d-oracle}), py312.\\
imports & \dn & after supplying \code{libGL} (conda-forge \code{libgl-devel}); the container is
headless and CompuCell3D links its graphics stack unconditionally.\\
pure-Python model definition & \dn & \code{cc3d.core.PyCoreSpecs} --- a simulation can be built
without XML, which is what makes a scriptable oracle possible at all.\\
seed exposed & \dn & \code{PottsCore(random\_seed=...)}, so determinism is at least
\emph{expressible}.\\
headless run & \textcolor{bad}{\textbf{BLOCKED}} & see below.\\
deterministic at a fixed seed & \ns & cannot be tested until it runs.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\textbf{The blocker is a bug in CompuCell3D 4.10.0, not in the plan.} Driving it from
\code{PyCoreSpecs} through \code{service\_cc3d} reaches
\code{persistent\_globals.get\_custom\_settings\_path()}, which does
\code{Path(custom\_settings\_source)} where \code{custom\_settings\_source} is the \emph{list of
specs} rather than a simulation file path:

\begin{center}
\small\code{TypeError: argument should be a str or an os.PathLike object ... not 'list'}
\end{center}

Three ways out, in the order they will be tried: an earlier release (4.9.0 / 4.8.0) where the
pure-Python path may predate the regression; the \code{.cc3d} project-file route via
\code{cc3d\_runScript.sh}, which is the officially supported headless entry point and does not
touch that code path; or setting the custom-settings path explicitly before the call.

\textbf{Nothing downstream can be trusted until this gate passes}, and that is the whole
discipline: jax-morph was chosen \emph{because} it ran, and the note said so. A target that cannot
be run reduces the atlas to the Okuda situation --- wrong operator, wrong parameter or wrong
reading, with no way to tell which.

\section{What is not started}

Phases 1--7. The mechanism inventory, the specs, the differential tests and the ledger all wait on
the oracle gate. \textbf{The one thing worth doing before it passes} is the instrument gap
\code{TRANSFER.md} identifies: the ledger's \code{implementation} class detects a contract claimed
twice \emph{within} one run, not \emph{across} runs --- so a CompuCell3D mechanism matching a
jax-morph \emph{candidate} would read as \code{new} when it is really a second sighting. That is a
defect that only becomes visible at the second repository, which is exactly the kind of thing this
exercise exists to surface.
