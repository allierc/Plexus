Operators

How state moves — grouped by family, in the visual language of the operator algebra

Each operator belongs to one of the elementary families of the operator algebra — lateral, aggregate, broadcast, exchange, field, rewire, structural (divide and die) and seed; the logo marks which. The runtime also exposes a field kind, a field’s own self-update.

Rewire \(\mathcal{R}\!:E\)

Rebuild the relation \(E\) each tick.

Structural \(|S|\)

Change the entities themselves — divide, die, grow.

no matrix particle may end a frame inside the tissue. cytokinetic_ringThe cytokinetic ring: the myosin a division leaves on the junction it just built, debited from the cortex that built it. ecm_gate_growthMechanosensitive growth: the matrix’s pressure slows the CELL CYCLE where it presses hardest, so the tissue grows into an anisotropic shape instead of being deformed into one. ecm_loadThe second half of the coupling: the matrix pushing back on the tissue, from a pressure map the matrix pass recorded. integrin_trackRide the fibres’ cell ends on the epithelial surface: a prescribed constraint on one row of particles, rather than on the grid. junction_myosinPer-junction myosin, recruited by tension: a stretched junction recruits more, pulls harder, and so shrinks – the surface_driveMove a seeded surface along one axis at a prescribed rate, and publish its velocity: the loading protocol of a displacement-controlled indentation. surface_trackWrite a recorded Field \(\partial_t\phi\)
