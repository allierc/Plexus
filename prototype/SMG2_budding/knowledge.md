`/workspace/Plexus/prototype/embryo_blastulla` is a **prior agentic loop that is NOT yet conclusive**
but is **rich with hundreds of authored specs and their paired outputs / analyses / scorecards**. It
climbed a ladder 1A→…→GRO→PAT→MOR→BUD→BRN→ORG on a *synthetic* blastula. Its later stages are exactly
our physics:
- **GRO** — continuous `cell_grow` (rest-volume increase, INDEPENDENT of division) expands tissue;
  anisotropic growth makes a protrusion that then ROUNDS by elastic + surface-tension relaxation.
- **BUD** — spatially-localized (pattern-gated) `cell_grow` → an emergent, non-scripted bud.
- **BRN** — a feedback law (`stress_gain`, tip inhibition, growth competition) turns one tip into
  multiple branches; **branching EMERGES, never a branch operator.**

adapt them here.** Reuse; do not restart from scratch. Key transferable lessons already established
there: division alone caps growth ~1.5× (it is mixing/repacking, not shape-making — GROWTH makes
shape); collapse is cured by lowering mechanical feedback gain, not by cranking `repel`; shape index

## 2. Literature → mechanism → operator map
**Core result (Okuda et al. 2018; Brückner & Hannezo 2025 review, `papers/Tissue_active_matter.pdf`):**
*"Combining Turing and 3D vertex models reproduces autonomous multicellular morphogenesis with
undulation, tubulation, and branching"* — coupling a **local morphogen level to cell behaviors
(growth, tension)** generically yields tubulation and branching. This is our target mechanism class.

| biological mechanism (lit) | Plexus operators |
|---|---|
| **Turing / RD morphogen pattern** gating growth (Okuda 2018; Iber lab: Gómez/Iber 2021, Kokic/Iber 2019) | `reaction_diffusion`, or `deposit`+`diffuse`+`decay` (activator/inhibitor channels) |
| **Morphogen-gated localized growth → bud** (Okuda 2018) | pattern field → gates `cell_grow` (anisotropic/tip) + `cell_divide` (repopulate) |
| **Keller–Segel contractile instability** (density↑→force↑→pulls cells→feedback; review Fig.) | `active_stress`/`agent_to_mpm` with density-dependent gain (`stress_gain`) |
| **Motility-driven unjamming + flocking** = collective migration (Bi 2015/16; Garcia/Hannezo/Gov 2015) | `motility`+`polar_align`+`repel` (shape-index fluidization); PIV-coherent flow |
| **Differential-growth buckling / folding** (Varner & Nelson 2015; "buckling without bending") | spatially-patterned `cell_grow` inside an elastic `mls_mpm_mechanics` medium |
| **Surface tension rounds buds; adhesion sets packing** (foam analogy) | `tension`, `adhesion`, `cohesion`, `separation` |
| **Hydraulic pressure / lumen** (tubulogenesis; Yang/Hannezo/Liberali 2021) | MPM pressure via `mpm_*` (later stage) |

## 3. THE TARGET — a SMOOTH constraint on exploration (real-data observables)
Generated by `real_data_montage.py` → `real_data_montage.png` (topology / growth / PIV over 4 times).
The forward model's OWN analyzed observables should approach these **smoothly** (soft penalties, not
hard integer gates — exploration is guided toward the phenomenology, not pinned to it):

- **budding trajectory** — `n_bud(t)` rises **monotonically and smoothly**, ~4→~20 (≈3–5× over the run):
  score `bud_trend` (Spearman ρ→+1) and `bud_growth_ratio` (→~5), not exact integers.
- **branching** — `n_branch` ~2→3 (few major bifurcations), `n_tube` ≡ 1 (one connected duct tree).
- **growth** — net population ≈ ×1.3; proliferation source (`∂ρ/∂t+∇·(ρv)`) **localized at bud tips**
  (`growth_tip_localization` high), smooth in space/time.
- **migration** — coherent PIV flow: nonzero `polar_order`, correlation length a few cell-diameters,
  tip-directed streaming (not jammed, not random).

**Smooth-target loss (soft):** a weighted sum of (1−ρ_bud) + |log(growth_ratio/5)| +
(1−tip_localization) + (1−polar_order_match) + branch/tube tolerance terms — minimize, don't threshold.
A slot is BETTER if it moves smoothly toward the target on ALL families without a hard failure
(collapse / escape / a scripted bud operator). NB the automated `n_bud` runs ~2–3× finer than the
coarse human GT (it resolves sub-lobules); calibrate the bud SCALE once with the annotator (Shaohe) —
until then, gate on the **trajectory shape**, not the absolute integer.

  velocity); growth = continuity residual of that PIV flow (separates production from advection).
