\begin{abstract}
\noindent
This is a build specification, not an essay. It defines an agentic loop that searches for the
\emph{mechanism} behind a morphogenetic phenotype --- as a composition of Plexus operators --- and
that is designed to run unattended on a GPU partition for weeks without descending into the
failure mode that consumed our last forty-four rounds.

The target system is Okuda tubulation: a closed epithelial shell that grows a tube. We already
make a tube; we make it \emph{by the wrong mechanism}, and we know this because relaxing our
tube toward mechanical equilibrium destroys it (\S\ref{sec:question}). That gives the campaign a
sharp, falsifiable objective rather than a resemblance target.

Section~\ref{sec:have} states what machinery already exists and transfers. Section~\ref{sec:space}
defines the Okuda composition space concretely --- operators, ports, stages, legal edits.
Section~\ref{sec:rabbit} diagnoses why searches like ours go into rabbit holes, from our own
record with numbers. Section~\ref{sec:control} is the core of the document: an explicit control
law with a batch--triage--truncate--starve--freeze--terminate cycle, so that budget is
\emph{taken away} from unproductive branches automatically. Section~\ref{sec:agents} assigns the
work to agents, borrowing orchestration from two multi-agent systems published together in
\emph{Nature} in July 2026 while deliberately diverging from their adjudication.
Sections~\ref{sec:out}--\ref{sec:diff} define the artefacts, including the causal descriptions
and the trigger that hands a problem to the differentiable fit. Section~\ref{sec:defects} is a
pre-flight checklist of eleven defects that must be closed first --- five of them found while
writing this document.
\end{abstract}

\section{The question the campaign answers}
\label{sec:question}

\subsection{Not ``can we make a tube''}

We can. A tip-tracking activation driver on a growing, dividing vesicle produces a long, thin,
constant-diameter tube with a length-to-diameter ratio above four, and its diameter tracks the
morphogen diffusion coefficient --- a qualitative reproduction of the published law. That
question is closed.

\subsection{The question is which mechanism holds it open}

Round 41 of that campaign asked what happens if the tissue is pushed toward the published
\emph{regime} --- Okuda's model is explicitly quasi-static, relaxing to force balance between
growth events. The answer was decisive:

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Relaxation destroys our tube.} More relaxation collapses it back toward a sphere. Our
tube is held open by an explicit outward force; remove the force and the shape energy pulls it
in. The published tube is an \emph{equilibrium} shape produced by localised proliferation.
\end{quote}

The mechanical signature agrees: force and pressure are concentrated entirely in the protrusion,
the body is idle, and division and migration pile up at the same tip. That is what a forced
protrusion looks like. A grown one shares load with the body.

So two mechanisms produce the same picture, and the picture is exactly what cannot tell them
apart. This is the general statement of why parameter search cannot find a mechanism, and it is
stronger than the usual one: \emph{the search optimises resemblance, and resemblance is the
quantity blind to the distinction that matters.}

\subsection{The campaign objective: a causal lever-map, not a target}

An earlier draft of this note stated the objective as one question --- \emph{produce a sustained
tube as a growth-driven quasi-static equilibrium}. That framing is wrong, and the throughput
arithmetic of \S\ref{sec:weeks} is what exposed it: with a single target the search
\emph{converges}, freezes every cluster within days, and stops, having established one fact.
It also wastes most of its own evidence, because a composition that does not make a tube teaches
nothing about tubes.

The objective is the one plexus2's Loop~I already names:

\begin{quote}\itshape
Build the CAUSAL LEVER-MAP of the mechanism space: for every operator, every implementation and
every routing, what does it do \emph{alone}, and what does it do \emph{in combination} --- since
mixtures rarely surrender their causal structure to inspection.
\end{quote}

The product is a map, not a winner. Specific questions become \textbf{queries against it}:

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{3.2cm}p{10.8cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{query} & \textbf{answered once the relevant cells are covered} \\
\midrule
sustained tube & a tube that survives relaxation, \emph{without} the extrusion forcing node \\
phase diagram & Okuda's four $(\chi,\gamma)$ morphologies --- thin tube, thick tube, undulation,
branching --- from \emph{one} composition \\
necessity & for each observed phenotype, which operators are load-bearing \\
impossibility & which composition families provably cannot reach a phenotype \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsection{The cells of the map, and coverage as progress}

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{2.2cm}p{11.8cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Solo} & for each operator and each structural implementation, its effect alone \\
\textbf{Pair} & for each pair, is the joint effect what the two solo effects predict
(\emph{additive}), or not? \textbf{Interactions are the expensive knowledge} --- they are
precisely what cannot be read off the code \\
\textbf{Routing} & morphogen $\to$ growth.\code{gate} vs $\to$ divide.\code{axis} vs $\to$
extrude.\code{site}: same operators, different mechanism \\
\textbf{Necessity} & for each observed phenotype, which ablations destroy it \\
\textbf{Phenotype} & which regions produce which morphology --- labels \emph{discovered} by the
analysts, never declared in advance \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Progress is then \textbf{coverage}: the fraction of cells carrying enough evidence to state a
verdict. Every run fills a cell, so no run is wasted, and the campaign has a natural extent ---
it runs until the levers are characterised. That is a weeks-scale programme rather than a
days-scale search, and it is what makes the multi-week campaign coherent rather than merely
long.

Two subordinate questions inherit from the record and enter the map as ordinary open cells
rather than as blockers: whether patterning can be \emph{emergent} (a late round concluded the
activator--inhibitor system floods the growing domain and cannot maintain a tip-riding peak ---
an impossibility claim made \emph{before} the precondition checks existed, so it re-enters as
\textbf{Open}), and whether per-cell three-dimensional volume changes the answer (the monolayer
implementation, whose first wiring spiked because division still triggered on the wedge volume
rather than $A\!\cdot\!h$).

\subsection{The metric that makes the objective measurable}

The objective above is useless without an observable that separates a forced tube from a grown
one. No metric in the existing bank does. We therefore define one, and it is cheap:

\paragraph{The quasi-static test.} Take a run's final state; continue the simulation with
\emph{growth and any driver switched off} and only mechanics and reconnection active; measure
what fraction of the protrusion survives.
\[
Q \;=\; \frac{\text{aspect after relaxation}}{\text{aspect before relaxation}} .
\]
A forced tube collapses ($Q \to 0$). An equilibrium tube persists ($Q \to 1$). Alongside it we
record the pressure ratio between tube and body, which should approach unity for a grown tube and
is presently around three.

$Q$ is the campaign's primary discriminator. It is defined before the search starts, it is
falsifiable, and it is the direct operationalisation of the round-41 finding. Authoring it is an
instance of the discipline this whole document is built around: \emph{when the eye sees a
distinction no number captures, the missing number is the deliverable.}

\section{What already exists and transfers}
\label{sec:have}

A working three-loop discovery system exists --- roughly 1{,}100 lines --- and has run unattended
for six rounds on embryonic gland branching, producing 1{,}216 archived runs and one conclusion
that a later, better measurement overturned. It is the substrate for this campaign. This section
records what transfers unchanged, what must be replaced, and what must be added.

\subsection{Transfers unchanged --- the three structural guarantees}

\paragraph{1. A composition is a typed graph, and its identity excludes parameters.}
A candidate mechanism is operator nodes plus typed connections between output ports and input
slots. A morphogen may be routed to a cleft \code{source}, a growth \code{gate}, a chemotaxis
\code{field} or an anisotropy \code{axis} --- four \emph{different mechanisms}, not four settings.
Identity is a hash of the structure only:

\begin{lstlisting}
def comp_hash(composition):
    # operators + typed connections ONLY -- no parameter values, no seed
\end{lstlisting}

Therefore \textbf{a change of numbers provably cannot register as a new hypothesis}. This single
line is the structural fix for what happened over rounds 1--30, and it is why the campaign can be
trusted to count its own progress honestly.

\paragraph{2. Evidence and interpretation are separate objects.}
The rule is enforced in code, not in prose:
\[
\text{simulation} \;\longrightarrow\; \text{RunRecord} \;\longrightarrow\; \text{knowledge}
\qquad \text{(never simulation}\longrightarrow\text{knowledge).}
\]
A \code{RunRecord} carries the raw facts of one simulation --- composition, parameters, seed,
backend, initial condition, parent, and the single edit that produced it --- and is
\emph{immutable}: assigning to a raw fact raises. Measurements attach as \emph{versioned,
append-only} analyses, so \code{metric\_v2} sits beside \code{metric\_v1} and neither is lost. The
archive is append-only, and run identity is a content hash of exactly the reproducible inputs, so
a crashed campaign resumes without duplicating or losing evidence.

This is what let a new observable, in the gland campaign, \textbf{re-score archived runs and
change a mechanistic conclusion without re-simulating anything}. It is the most valuable property
the system has.

\paragraph{3. One legal edit at a time, stage-gated.}
Four moves exist --- add, remove, connect, disconnect --- each returning a new graph together with
the edit that produced it. Every run records its parent and its mutation, so the archive is a
causal tree, not a bag of configurations. Stage gating keeps the legal move set small and
interpretable rather than an enumeration of $2^N$ subsets.

\subsection{Must be replaced}

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{4.6cm}p{9.9cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Component} & \textbf{Why it must change} \\
\midrule
Operator vocabulary & The existing one is phase-field gland vocabulary. Okuda needs its own
(\S\ref{sec:space}). \\
Frontier expansion & Breadth-first with a node cap, spending budget evenly on good and hopeless
branches. Replaced by the control law of \S\ref{sec:control}. \\

operators. Replaced by an earned claim (\S\ref{sec:defects}, defect~8). \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsection{Must be added}

The supervisor, the cheap-tier triage, the proximity starvation rule, the watcher, the causal
interpreter, and the artefact pipeline --- all specified below.

\section{The Okuda composition space}
\label{sec:space}

The space is defined by an operator vocabulary with typed ports, a stage gate, and a seed. All
operators below are already registered and exercised; none is hypothetical.

\subsection{Vocabulary}

\textbf{Stage} & \textbf{Operator} & \textbf{Kind} & \textbf{Ports} \\
\midrule

\midrule
\multirow{4}{*}{2 --- growth \& topology}

\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Two design choices in that table are load-bearing.

\paragraph{Extrusion is an operator node, not a parameter.}
The whole round-41 finding is ``ablate the forcing and the tube collapses''. If the forcing lives
inside a configuration file it can only be turned down; if it is a node in the graph, the
necessity test \textbf{ablates it automatically}, on every composition, as part of the standard
protocol. The campaign's central question thereby becomes a routine measurement rather than a
special experiment.

\paragraph{Three reaction kinetics are three implementations of one contract.}
\op{cell\_react} already resolves to \code{gray\_scott}, \code{gierer\_meinhardt} and
\code{brusselator}. Swapping them is an implementation choice under a single biological contract,
which is what the language claims and here is simply true. The search can therefore ask ``which
kinetics'' without changing the composition's identity, and ask ``with or without reaction at
all'' as a genuine structural edit.

\subsection{Seed and legal edits}

The seed is Stage 1 only: a mesh, a shape energy, and reconnection --- a relaxing vesicle that
does nothing. Every subsequent mechanism is reached by one legal edit. Crucially the search
visits the degenerate corners on the way (growth with no patterning, patterning with no growth,
mechanics alone), and those are where impossibility results come from.

\section{Why searches like ours go into rabbit holes}
\label{sec:rabbit}

Every defect in \S\ref{sec:defects} is fixable in days. The harder problem is that a search can be
free of bugs and still waste months. Our own record supplies the diagnosis, and the pathology has
five separable components --- worth separating because each needs a different countermeasure.

\begin{enumerate}\itemsep3pt
\item \textbf{Depth-first drift.} Three separate rounds tested, in substance, ``the same thing but
more'': run longer, activate a bigger patch, make the wall thicker. All three were falsified. Each
was a locally reasonable next step from the configuration in front of us. None was a new
hypothesis. A search that always expands its current best node does this forever.

\item \textbf{No terminal-state rule.} Nothing ever said ``this branch is finished''. Rounds
continued because there was always another knob. Stopping was a human losing patience, which is
not a criterion.

\item \textbf{Near-duplicate proliferation.} Thirty rounds explored perhaps four genuinely
distinct ideas. Without a distance between hypotheses, twenty variants of one idea consume twenty
times the budget of one idea --- and their collective failure is then misread as strong evidence
against the idea, rather than as one observation repeated.

\item \textbf{The eye and the number disagreeing, undetected.} Two of the most confident
intermediate conclusions were measurement artefacts: a ``97\% of cells inverted'' result produced
by pairing each frame's coordinates with a different frame's connectivity, and a ``101 chemical
spots'' count where the eye saw three, because the threshold sat inside the noise. Both were
caught by looking at a picture. Neither by a number. In a supervised session a human notices; in
an unattended campaign nobody does.

\item \textbf{The human as sole goal-holder.} An agent elaborates whatever is in front of it and
cannot detect its own drift. Every course correction on record --- ``we are meant to be testing
the growth-driven mechanism, not tuning this extrusion'' --- came from outside the loop.
\end{enumerate}

The rest of this document is, in effect, five countermeasures.

\bottomrule

\subsection{Termination and escalation}

The supervisor owns the campaign configuration --- objective, success criteria, stopping rule,
budget --- and workers cannot amend it. Each round it computes:

\begin{lstlisting}
if objective_met(ledger):            terminate("objective")
if all_clusters_frozen():            escalate()      # widen stage gate, or request an operator
if dry_rounds >= 2:                  escalate()      # no new cluster AND no best-score gain
if budget_spent >= budget:           terminate("budget")
\end{lstlisting}

\emph{Escalate} is the important branch, and it is what a human did by hand last time. It means:
the current space is exhausted, so either open the next stage gate, or record an
\textbf{operator request} --- a mechanism the proposer wanted and could not express --- and move
on. Escalation is logged and is a deliverable (\S\ref{sec:out}).

\subsection{Rules enforced in code, not recommended in prose}

\begin{itemize}\itemsep2pt
\item \textbf{A change of numbers is never a new hypothesis} --- guaranteed by the structural hash.
\item \textbf{No manual parameter tweaking outside the loop.} If a configuration needs hand-tuning
to work, that is evidence of a missing operator, not of a missing afternoon.
\item \textbf{No silent caps.} Any round that truncates, samples, or drops candidates says so in
the log with counts. A silent truncation reads afterwards as complete coverage.
\item \textbf{Structural limitations are recorded once and never retried.}
\item \textbf{Per-round compute is bounded.} Depth is traded against basin width inside a fixed
budget, and the trade is recorded. The current driver deepens every four rounds with no ceiling,
which silently converts a campaign of many cheap experiments into a few enormous ones.
\end{itemize}

\section{The scientific protocol: hypothesis first}
\label{sec:protocol}

The control law of \S\ref{sec:control} says how budget is allocated. This section says what makes
an allocation an \emph{experiment} rather than a sample. One rule, enforced in code:

\begin{quote}
\textbf{A candidate is not run until its prediction has been recorded.}
\end{quote}

Every candidate carries, before it reaches the cluster: a falsifiable \emph{claim}, the single
\emph{metric} that decides it, an explicit \emph{prediction} on that metric, a \emph{rationale},
and the Grounder's \emph{citations}. A hypothesis without a prediction is refused at construction.

\subsection{Two intents, and why the mixture matters}

Each batch is allocated between two kinds of edit:

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{2.6cm}p{11.6cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Confirmatory} & an edit the current causal map predicts \emph{will} work. Consolidates
the map. Individually low-information; collectively what makes the map usable. \\
\textbf{Adversarial} & an edit predicted to \emph{break} the current best explanation.
High-variance; this is where surprise lives. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Because the prediction precedes the run, every outcome falls into a $2\times2$:

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
 & \textbf{confirmed} & \textbf{refuted} \\
\midrule
\textbf{confirmatory} & consolidates (low information) & \textbf{surprise} (high information) \\
\textbf{adversarial}  & \textbf{surprise} (high information) & breaks as expected (low information) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

A batch of pure confirmation ($100/0$) only ever confirms what we already believe: near-zero
information. A batch of pure falsification ($0/100$) only ever probes the improbable, which mostly
breaks as expected and never consolidates a working map. The productive operating point is a
deliberate minority of attacks on the current understanding --- roughly $70/30$, and at times
$50/50$ when the map is young.

\subsection{The 70/30 balance is a setpoint, not a quota}

This is the part that makes the discipline machine-enforceable. Define the \emph{surprise rate}
of a round as the fraction of resolved hypotheses whose outcome contradicted its prediction. Then
the Supervisor closes a loop on it:

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{surprise} & \textbf{diagnosis} & \textbf{correction} \\
\midrule
$< 0.10$ & drifted to $100/0$: confirming what we already believe & push \textbf{adversarial} \\
$\approx 0.30$ & the productive band & hold $70/30$ \\
$> 0.50$ & drifted to $0/100$: everything breaks, no map accumulates & push \textbf{confirmatory} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

A quota cannot notice that its questions have become trivial; a setpoint on the prediction-error
rate can. This is the mechanism by which the loop avoids both failure modes the hand-run campaign
fell into --- a long stretch of confirmatory tuning where nothing surprised us, followed by a
stretch of wild attempts that mostly broke as expected.

\subsection{The knowledge document}

\code{knowledge.md} is append-only and written in the order the science happened: the hypothesis
and its grounding, then its prediction, then what occurred. A reader can audit what was believed
\emph{before} the evidence arrived --- which is the only way to tell a prediction from a
post-hoc rationalisation. Verdicts move between the four classes of \S\ref{sec:out}; a claim's
history is extended, never rewritten. Surprises are flagged, because they are the entries a
returning human should read first.

\section{The agents}
\label{sec:agents}

Two systems published together in \emph{Nature} (July 2026) solve much of the orchestration
problem for literature-driven hypothesis generation with wet-lab validation: Co-Scientist, a
supervisor-coordinated coalition with an Elo tournament, a proximity graph and a meta-review that
appends learned patterns to other agents' prompts; and Robin, which batches and truncates,
ranks by an LLM-judged pairwise tournament aggregated with a Bradley--Terry--Luce model, and runs
eight independent analysis trajectories reconciled by consensus.

Our setting differs in one decisive respect. \textbf{Their experiment is scarce and ours is
cheap.} A wet-lab assay costs weeks, so ranking by argument is rational. Our simulation costs
seconds and is measured by code. We therefore take their orchestration, diverge on their
adjudication, and add one agent neither has.

\paragraph{The Grounder, specifically.} It is worth being precise about this agent because it
is the one whose absence was most expensive last time. The Grounder holds three bodies of text ---
\code{paper/plexus2.tex} (what the language permits), \code{papers/okuda.pdf} (what the reference

\code{papers/} corpus (what the field has already established) --- and it is called at exactly
three points:

\begin{enumerate}\itemsep2pt
\item when the \textbf{Proposer} needs a mechanism grounded before proposing it;
\item when the \textbf{Supervisor} gates a hypothesis, before Discovery is allowed to propose;
\item when an \textbf{operator request} is filed --- does the literature already name this
mechanism, and under what conditions does it hold?
\end{enumerate}

This is the agent that produced the Gierer--Meinhardt unlock by hand last time. The decisive
question --- \emph{which reaction--diffusion regime does Okuda actually use?} --- was answered by
reading the paper, not by sweeping parameters, and it redirected the entire campaign. Making that
a scheduled call rather than a lucky afternoon is the point.

Robin used different models for generation and adjudication, and the separation is free. And
Robin found their orchestrator ``almost always called tools in the same order'', so they replaced
the agentic controller with a deterministic notebook. We follow that: \textbf{the control law of
\S\ref{sec:control} is a deterministic script}, and language models are called only where
judgement is genuinely required --- proposing, watching, explaining.

\paragraph{One thing neither paper needs.} Their measurement is an external assay. Ours is code
written by the same system being judged, so ``the metric lied'' is a failure mode unique to us,
and we have suffered it twice. Hence the instrument gate of \S\ref{sec:defects}.

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\section{Knowledge certification, and who may touch the substrate}
\label{sec:certify}

\begin{quote}
\emph{If the foundations are wrong, so is the knowledge.}
\end{quote}

Every other agent in \S\ref{sec:agents} produces or judges \emph{evidence}. None of them is
responsible for the thing evidence is measured \emph{with}. That gap is not hypothetical: it is
how the defect below was found --- by a person reading code, not by any designed role.

\subsection{A worked example of why the role must exist}

\code{divide\_3d} counts \code{min\_cycle} and \code{max\_cycle} in \emph{division-calls}, and
\code{max\_div} / \code{max\_div\_frac} are \emph{per-call} throttles. The archived configurations
passed \code{every: 2}, which the engine gated \emph{and} the operator's own private counter gated
--- so the operator body executed once every \textbf{four} frames. Correcting the clock therefore
multiplied the wall-clock meaning of every per-call quantity by four, and the same recipe that had
produced a tube of aspect $7.5$ produced one of aspect $3.2$.

Two further consequences, each of which a casual fix would have missed:

\begin{itemize}\itemsep2pt
\item The hand-tuned values had been adopted as \emph{vocabulary defaults}, on the reasoning that
``a default composition should start where our evidence is''. After the clock fix that reasoning
inverted: those defaults now encode a $\theta$ tuned for the wrong clock, so \emph{every}
composition the loop generates would have started biased --- silently.
\item Rescaling the obvious knob was not enough. Since
$\text{cap} = \max(\code{max\_div},\, \code{max\_div\_frac}\cdot n_F)$, the absolute floor
\emph{dominates} at realistic cell counts, so rescaling only \code{max\_div\_frac} was
\textbf{entirely masked}. Both had to move.
\end{itemize}

Because the factor is exact, the correction is analytic rather than a re-tuning sweep: rescale the
per-call quantities and the fix becomes \emph{behaviour-preserving by construction}, verified
identical to the archived budget at every cell count. Any later change in phenotype is then
attributable to the change that caused it, rather than to the clock.

\subsection{The Metrologist}

The Metrologist certifies that evidence may be admitted at all. It owns the validation ladder, the
instrument gate, and --- the part no other role covers --- \emph{substrate semantics}: units,
clocks, and the distinction between per-call and per-frame quantities. Its distinguishing power is
that it may act \emph{backwards}:

\begin{quote}
When a foundation defect is found, the Metrologist issues a \textbf{retraction}: every claim whose
evidence depends on the defective semantics is moved back to \emph{Open}, with the defect and its
scope recorded.
\end{quote}

In an append-only ledger a retraction is a new record, never an edit. The history must show what
was believed, on what evidence, and why that evidence was later withdrawn --- otherwise a reader
cannot distinguish a claim that survived scrutiny from one that was quietly repaired.

\subsection{The boundary: who may modify the substrate}

\begin{quote}
\textbf{No campaign agent may modify the substrate.} An agent that can rewrite the simulator can
make any hypothesis true.
\end{quote}

The separation is therefore structural, not procedural:

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{2.6cm}p{11.6cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Metrologist} & \emph{detects and quarantines.} Halts admission of evidence, marks affected
claims Open-pending-recheck, and states the scope of the defect. It does not write code. \\
\textbf{Engineer} & \emph{proposes a patch}, into the substrate --- a \emph{different artefact}
from the campaign's evidence. It cannot admit its own fix. \\
\textbf{Supervisor} & \emph{gates resumption.} Evidence admission restarts only after the full
ladder passes on the patched substrate, and the re-anchoring is recorded explicitly rather than
absorbed. \\
\textbf{Human} & approves substrate changes. The campaign runs unattended; the thing it measures
with does not change unattended. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The underlying principle is the one an experimentalist would recognise: \emph{the instrument must
not be adjustable by the experiment it is measuring.} Our situation is sharper than either
published system's, because their measurement is an external wet-lab assay and ours is code
written by the same project that is being judged.

\section{Which agents are language models, and which must not be}
\label{sec:whichllm}

Co-Scientist runs six specialised LLM agents under a mechanical supervisor: Generation,
Reflection (peer review \emph{with search}), Ranking (Elo by simulated scientific debate),
Proximity, Evolution (refines the top-ranked) and Meta-review (distils patterns and appends them
to the other agents' prompts --- ``feedback propagation without back-propagation''). Robin uses
Crow and Falcon for literature and Finch for analysis, ranks with an LLM judge over pairwise
comparisons aggregated by Bradley--Terry--Luce, and runs \emph{eight independent} Finch
trajectories on the same data because the analysis ``can vary between runs, even when given
identical prompts and data''.

We follow that, with two deliberate departures and one addition.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{2.4cm}p{1.9cm}p{9.6cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Agent} & \textbf{LLM?} & \textbf{Why} \\
\midrule
Proposer     & \textbf{yes} & Chooses which mechanism edit is worth a GPU-hour and commits to a
prediction. An enumerator cannot do this: if the prediction follows mechanically from the edit's
sign, the surprise rate measures nothing. \\
Reflection   & \textbf{yes} & Peer-reviews the batch before it costs cluster time. A type-correct
proposal can still be a bad experiment. \\
Analyst $\times N$ & \textbf{yes} & $N$ independent readings of one run, reconciled by consensus;
disagreement is recorded as a calibration signal. \\
Watcher      & \textbf{yes} (VLM) & Reads the movie and \emph{vetoes} promotion when the picture
contradicts the claim. \\
Interpreter  & \textbf{yes} & Writes the causal account --- this spec, this phenomenon, by this
route. \\
Evolution    & \textbf{yes} & Refines the winner (simplify / synthesise / ground). \\
Meta-review  & \textbf{yes} & Distils recurring patterns into the Proposer's instructions. \\

\midrule
Supervisor   & no & Arithmetic on statistics. An LLM here is drift, not judgement. \\
Critic       & no & The type and precondition guard. It is the thing that stops a language model

Referee      & no & Ranks by \emph{measuring}. \\

\paragraph{Departure 1: ranking is measured \emph{and} judged.}
Both papers rank by argument because a wet-lab assay is scarce. Ours is a simulation scored by
code, so an opinion about available ground truth is a step backwards --- \emph{except} that the
instrument gate has now measured a metric scoring $9.30$ on a small bud. So the metric bank ranks
first, an LLM judge ranks independently from the rendered movie, and \textbf{their disagreement
is the signal}: it is the eye/number divergence detector. Neither paper needs this, because
their measurement is external to their system; ours is code we wrote.

\paragraph{Departure 2: pre-registration.}
Neither system records what was \emph{believed before} the evidence arrived, so a prediction
cannot be distinguished from a post-hoc rationalisation. Ours refuses to run a candidate whose
prediction is not already recorded (\S\ref{sec:protocol}).

\paragraph{Addition: the type system is not an agent.}
Illegal edits, unmet preconditions and dangling slots never reach the Proposer at all --- it
chooses from a menu the type system has already filtered. The language decides what is
\emph{possible}; the agent decides what is \emph{worth doing}. Neither can do the other's job.

\section{Running for weeks: where this breaks}
\label{sec:weeks}

The campaign is meant to run unattended for weeks. That is a different engineering problem from
running one round, and the arithmetic should be done before the fact rather than discovered.

One run is roughly $250\,$s of engine, $60\,$s of rendering, $60\,$s of captioning and a
mechanics pass: call it \textbf{7 minutes}. Eight-way on the partition gives $\sim$68 runs per
hour. A round is a batch of eight plus a proposer call, so $\sim$20 minutes, or $\sim$70 rounds
per day. Two weeks is then of order $10^3$ rounds and $10^4$ runs.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.4cm}p{4.0cm}p{9.4cm}@{}}
\toprule
 & \textbf{Bottleneck} & \textbf{Consequence, and what it forces} \\
\midrule
1 & \textbf{The composition space is too small.} & Twelve operators, $\sim$19 legal edits from
the seed. Distinct \emph{clusters} number in the tens. At eight runs a round the search
\textbf{exhausts itself and freezes every cluster within days}. A multi-week campaign is
therefore \emph{not} mechanism search --- it is dominated by parameter fitting and by
\textbf{operator requests}. Escalation is the main path, not an edge case, and it is the least
built part of the system. \\
2 & VLM cost & A $23\,$GB model loaded once per job. Caption per \emph{wave} instead: one load,
eight captions. Still always-caption, never deferred to an end pass. \\
3 & Storage & $30$--$50\,$MB per trajectory $\times\,10^4$ runs $\approx 400\,$GB. Retention
policy: full trajectories for kept and promoted runs, per-frame metric tables (kilobytes) for
everything else --- so the archive stays re-scorable exactly where re-scoring matters. \\
4 & The proposer call blocks & Eight GPUs idle for minutes every round. Propose round $N{+}1$
while $N$ runs. \\
5 & \textbf{Knowledge growth is unmeasured} & The point of the campaign is that knowledge
accumulates, and there was no quantity tracking it. See below. \\
6 & The videos do not accumulate & Each run has its own movie; nothing assembles them into
something you can watch. See below. \\
7 & Prompt growth & Meta-review appends forever. The distilled section is capped and rewritten
in place, so old patterns are dropped rather than accreted. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsection{Knowledge growth, as a measured quantity}

If the campaign's product is knowledge, then its rate is the thing to watch, and it should be
plotted rather than asserted. Per round we record: distinct clusters discovered and frozen;
counts moving between Established / Refuted / Structural / Open; the \textbf{surprise rate}
(\S\ref{sec:protocol}); operator requests filed; and the fraction of runs admitted as evidence
at all. A campaign whose surprise rate collapses toward zero has stopped learning even while it
is still burning GPU-hours --- and that, not a wall-clock limit, is the honest signal to
escalate or stop.

\subsection{The video battery}

Every run already produces a movie, a strip, a caption and a mechanics panel. What was missing
is that they never assembled. Three artefacts accumulate across the campaign:

\begin{itemize}\itemsep2pt
\item a \textbf{round montage} --- the batch's final frames side by side, so a round is legible
at a glance;
\item a \textbf{progress reel} --- the best run of every round, concatenated in order, so the
campaign can be \emph{watched} learning;
\item a \textbf{phase-diagram panel} --- the four Okuda cells (\S\ref{sec:question}) as they
currently stand, which is simultaneously the scientific target and the talk figure.
\end{itemize}

The reel is not decoration. The single most expensive failure in this project's history was a
number disagreeing with a picture, and the cheapest defence has repeatedly been to look.

\section{What the campaign produces}
\label{sec:out}

Five artefacts, each a deliverable in its own right.

\subsection{The ledger}

Every claim sorted into \textbf{Established}, \textbf{Refuted}, \textbf{Structural limitation} or
\textbf{Open}, with its evidence, sample count, confidence interval and the threshold set that
produced the verdict. A mechanism reaches Established only if it answers what a biology paper
would ask: remove it --- does the phenotype collapse? Is a minimal recipe containing it
sufficient? Does it survive repeats and a range of settings?

\subsection{Causal descriptions --- ``this spec gives this phenomenon''}

For every surviving composition the Interpreter writes a structured account, attached to the
record as a versioned analysis:

\begin{lstlisting}
{ composition:  <structural hash + operator list + typed connections>,
  phenotype:    <class, with the metric vector that assigns it>,
  route:        "growth gated by the activator raises target volume locally;
                 oriented division builds a wall along the bud axis; T1 lets
                 the wall cells slide, so the tube extends instead of bulging",
  necessary:    [ops whose ablation collapses the phenotype],
  sufficient:   <the minimal sub-composition that still produces it>,
  falsified:    [alternatives tried and refuted, with run ids],
  scope:        <substrate, parameter ranges, seeds>,
  watcher:      <caption of the movie>,  agreement: <caption vs metrics> }
\end{lstlisting}

This is what makes the operator library reusable rather than merely catalogued, and it is what a
reader of the eventual paper actually wants. It is generated per composition, continuously, not
written up afterwards.

\subsection{The operator backlog --- atlas growth}

Every escalation records an \textbf{operator request}: a mechanism the proposer wanted and the
language could not express. Each is a candidate new operator and each is a talk bullet. Our
expectation, from the one simulator decomposed in full, is that mechanics \emph{saturate} against
the existing algebra while topology and numerics contribute genuinely new contracts --- and that
saturation is itself the hypothesis under test. If genuinely new families keep appearing, the
language is incomplete; if they stop, the compactness claim is supported.

\subsection{Movies}

Every promoted composition is rendered to the standard of the public gallery, captioned by the
watcher, and archived beside its record. Captioning is on by default and is never disabled: the
caption is simultaneously documentation, a semantic regression test, and the watcher's input.
These are the primary evidence for a talk, and the loop produces them rather than a person
producing them afterwards.

\section{Where the differentiable version enters}
\label{sec:diff}

Mechanism search and parameter fitting must not run simultaneously, or neither conclusion is
interpretable. The fit is \emph{earned}, and the trigger is explicit:

\begin{quote}
A composition that is \textbf{Established} but whose residual on a chosen objective persists
\emph{across the whole parameter basin} is handed to the differentiable fit. A residual that
survives every setting is evidence about a mechanism; a residual that disappears at some setting
was never a mechanism question.
\end{quote}

\paragraph{The objective is local in space and time.} A loss spanning every cell over hundreds of
frames unrolls, under reverse-mode differentiation, into a graph whose memory scales as cells
$\times$ steps and rapidly becomes intractable. But the evidence constraining any single operator
is usually confined to a small, transient region --- the advancing front, the neck, the moment a
wall forms. So the loss is placed exactly there: a region of interest over a few tens of steps.
The mechanism is global and shared; the objective is not.

\paragraph{The natural objective here is the stress field.} The tool that computes per-cell force,
pressure and cortical tension is already torch-differentiable, and the campaign's discriminator
$Q$ is mechanical. ``Minimise tube pressure while preserving aspect'' is therefore both the
biological question (is a low-stress tube reachable?) and a well-posed differentiable objective.

\paragraph{Deliverable.} One figure: residual descent, with identifiable parameters distinguished
from degenerate ones. A degeneracy found is a result --- it says two mechanisms are
indistinguishable under the current observable, which is a measurement question, and it goes back
to the observable bank.

\section{Pre-flight: eleven defects that must be closed first}
\label{sec:defects}

The campaign will emit scientific claims, including claims that something is \emph{impossible}. A
false impossibility claim is worse than no claim, because it closes a question that should stay
open. Defects 1--6 come from an adversarial audit of the simulation operators; 7--11 were found
while writing this document, by reading the loop's own source with the same suspicion.

\paragraph{1. Two operators kept a private clock.} They spaced themselves out while the engine
\emph{also} spaced them out; the schedules multiplied, so ``every 2 steps'' fired every 4.
Division across every archived run happened at a fraction of the advertised rate. \emph{Fix:
the engine owns the clock; baselines re-measured and the re-anchoring stated, not absorbed.}

\paragraph{2. Adding a signalling step silently changed the time-step.} The run script chose the
time-step from which operators were present --- recipes with a chemical signal ran fifty times
finer. Since the campaign's most important edit is adding or removing signalling, every
conclusion about whether signalling matters would have been confounded. \emph{Fix: one time-step
for the whole search; stability handled inside the operator that needs it.}

\paragraph{3. A silent mismatch produced a result we believed for days.} Positions and
connectivity were recorded on different strides; the analysis paired coordinates with another
moment's connectivity and reported cells as inverted. Every physical cure failed --- in hindsight,
the clue. \emph{Fix: equal length asserted, not clamped.}

\paragraph{4. An operator that does nothing still returns numbers.} If a prerequisite is missing,
the operator silently no-ops, the run finishes, and a score is produced. Harmless under
hand-written recipes; under automatic search it is the most dangerous bug possible --- the loop
records ``this mechanism cannot make tubes'' when the mechanism never ran. \emph{Fix:
preconditions declared and checked; every operator reports whether it acted; a scheduled operator
that never acted is an error, not a result.}

\paragraph{5. Some operators advertise capabilities they lack.} One is labelled differentiable and
discards gradients; another claims three dimensions while its validity check is two-dimensional.
Labels that lie are worse than absent labels, because the search trusts them. \emph{Fix:
corrected and mechanically certified.}

\paragraph{6. Measurements can pass while the picture is wrong.} A lumpy blob scored acceptably on
every number we had, because the scores measured how far things stick out and a blob sticks out.
\emph{Mitigation: the watcher, plus the instrument gate below.}

\paragraph{7. \textcolor{cop}{The archive stores only the final frame.}} Each run computes several
recorded frames and persists only the last, so every re-scoring is restricted to end-state
observables. This contradicts the entire record: the decisive evidence was temporal --- a
protrusion that peaks mid-run then degrades, a pattern that floods only once growth starts,
divisions arriving in synchronised waves. Worse, the artefact that cost us days is by construction
a time-series defect, and an archive of final frames cannot be re-examined for it. \emph{Fix
before any long run: persist the full trajectory and a per-frame metric table. Storage is cheap;
re-simulating weeks of cluster time to recover a time course we already computed and discarded is
not.}

\paragraph{8. \textcolor{cop}{Impossibility is asserted by a hard-coded narrative.}} The strongest
claim the system makes is currently produced by a low score plus an \code{if/elif} chain guessing
a reason from absent operators. The reason is a template, not evidence; combined with defect 4
this is machinery for manufacturing confident false impossibility. \emph{Fix: an impossibility
claim requires (i) every scheduled operator verified to have acted, (ii) a minimum sample count
with an explicit interval, (iii) a recorded, bounded falsification attempt --- a deliberate search
for any setting that does produce the phenotype --- and (iv) a stated scope. Absent any of the
four, the verdict is Open.}

\paragraph{9. \textcolor{cop}{Four samples cannot support a robustness claim.}} The default basin
is two seeds by two parameter draws, so a rate is one of $\{0,\frac14,\frac12,\frac34,1\}$, and
promotion thresholds are hard-coded constants --- Established at $\ge0.6$ (three of four),
structural below $0.2$ (zero of four), necessary if ablation drops below $0.5$. \emph{Fix: a
minimum sample count before promotion; a Wilson interval rather than a point rate; thresholds as
named, recorded campaign parameters so a later re-analysis can vary them.}

\paragraph{10. The search accelerator is dead code in the long-run driver.} Loop~I accepts an
optional surrogate that ranks candidate edits so the frontier expands best-first; the overnight
driver never passes it. Every unattended round to date has been uniform breadth-first --- the exact
opposite of \S\ref{sec:control}.

\paragraph{11. Per-round cost grows without bound.} The driver deepens the search every four
rounds and densifies the basin every twelve, with no ceiling.

\subsection*{The instrument gate}

Before the first round, the metric bank must correctly separate a set of already-archived runs we
have labelled by eye --- a clean tube, a capped lobe, a flooded shell, a uniform sphere, a
cauliflower. If the instrument cannot reproduce our own judgement on known cases, it is not ready
to judge unknown ones. This is not a formality: three metrics in the existing bank have been
caught lying, one of which moved a headline number by thirty per cent once corrected.

\subsection*{The validation ladder}

One command, each level independently pass/fail, non-zero exit on any failure.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Level} & \textbf{Checks} & \textbf{Error class caught} \\
\midrule
Contract    & every operator declares what it is and does & labels that lie \\
Unit        & each operator reproduces its previous output & the refactor changed physics \\
Trajectory  & a run reproduces its recorded history & schedule or ordering drift \\
Invariants  & closed surface, no tangled or bow-tie faces & silent geometric corruption \\
Recording   & positions and connectivity are equal length & the phantom-result bug \\
Metrics     & scores match archived values & the analysis path drifted \\
Gradients   & derivatives flow and are finite & the fit will fail later \\
Coverage    & every operator exercised by some configuration & untested code presumed working \\
Description & the watcher's caption matches expectation & the numbers pass, the picture is wrong \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

One rung deserves comment. A face must not be self-intersecting, and we had checked validity by
requiring positive area. \emph{A bow-tie has positive area.} The check passed, the mesh was
tangled, and a quantitative result about cell rearrangement was wrong --- caught by watching a
movie, not by any number. The lesson generalises well beyond meshes: a numerical invariant is not
the geometric one you meant.

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\section{Operating it}

The partition is cheap enough that wall-clock is not the binding constraint --- attention is. The
operating rules follow from that.

\begin{itemize}\itemsep2pt
\item \textbf{Read the ledger, not the runs.} The weekly unit of human attention is the ledger
delta --- what moved between the four classes --- plus a montage of the week's best and the
current operator backlog. Individual runs are opened only when the ledger points at one.
\item \textbf{Verify against the world, never against a submission's reply.} A submission that
reports failure may have succeeded, and one that appears to time out may already be queued; we
have created duplicate jobs this way. The queue is the only ground truth. Generalised: an action's
\emph{reported} outcome is a hint; the world's state is the fact.
\item \textbf{Every round is restartable.} Evidence is persisted as produced, exceptions caught per
round, run identity a content hash --- so a crash resumes without duplicating or losing evidence.
\item \textbf{The configuration is written before the first round}, not discovered during it.
\end{itemize}

\section{Status and the switch-on gate}

The loop has run on gland branching and produced, unattended, a sequence that included one
conclusion overturned by a better measurement --- the strongest evidence that the
evidence/interpretation separation is worth its cost. The Okuda operators exist, have produced a
tube, and have produced the more interesting negative result that our tube is forced where the
published one is grown. That is the question the campaign inherits, and \S\ref{sec:question}
turns it into a measurable one.

The campaign switches on when four things are true:

\begin{enumerate}\itemsep2pt
\item the eleven defects are closed, each demonstrated by a test that fails loudly;
\item the archive stores trajectories and per-frame metrics, not final frames;
\item the metric bank has reproduced our own eye-labels on already-archived runs;
\item the supervisor's campaign configuration --- objective, criteria, stopping rule, budget --- is
written down.
\end{enumerate}

That gate is the point of this document. The system is now capable of running unattended for a
long time and producing confident, well-formatted, entirely wrong conclusions. The defence is not
more compute. It is a small number of checks that fail loudly, an archive that keeps raw evidence
separate from its interpretation, a search that drops its losers instead of lovingly refining
them, a supervisor that holds the goal nobody else can be trusted to hold --- and the willingness
to keep looking at the pictures.

\section{First live results (2026-07-30, overnight)}
\label{sec:live}

Three things were exercised for the first time: the LLM Proposer, the composition mode end to end,
and the loop pointed at a \emph{second} system (the Turing\,$\times$\,vertex recipe behind the
site's front-page movies). Each produced defects that only running could find.

\subsection{The composition mode could never have completed a round}

\texttt{proposer.propose()} \emph{rejects} any proposal whose slot 0 is not
\texttt{intent: "control"}; \texttt{hypothesis.INTENTS} did not contain \texttt{"control"}, so
\texttt{Hypothesis.\_\_post\_init\_\_} raised on it. Two halves of the same protocol disagreed
about whether a control exists and both enforced their view with a hard error. A control is now a
first-class intent, excluded from the surprise rate and the mix --- it asserts nothing about an
edit --- but a control that \emph{fails} its own prediction raises \textsc{baseline drift}, which
is louder than a surprise: every other slot is measured as a difference from it.

\subsection{The surprise rate was computed by a regex that could only bias it downwards}

The campaign's one control signal was a first-match regular expression with three defects: an
\emph{unparseable} prediction returned true and was recorded \texttt{confirmed}; the parser was
metric-blind, applying the first comparison it found to \texttt{protr\_peak} whatever the clause
named; and \texttt{REFUTED if \ldots} was read as the assertion. All three push the rate toward
``nothing was learned''.

The measured cost: the \texttt{vcap} round's five points were posed before the parameter-hypothesis
fix with the placeholder \texttt{"unknown -- sensitivity sweep"}, which parsed to nothing and so
resolved \texttt{confirmed}. The persisted surprise rate for the campaign's most informative round
was \textbf{0.00}. The 0.40 everyone quoted had been computed by hand in a narrative and never
reached the ledger. \texttt{predict.py} now parses every clause with its metric and returns
\texttt{inconclusive} rather than guessing; an append-only \texttt{amend()} backfilled the record,
and the ledger computes 0.40 itself. The same guard later refused to score 32 runs of the
Turing\,$\times$\,vertex study because \texttt{protr} was not a known metric --- declining 32 times
instead of inventing 32 confirmations.

\subsection{The supervisor did not survive a restart}

\texttt{round}, \texttt{spent}, \texttt{dry}, \texttt{best}, \texttt{prox} and \texttt{stage\_gate}
were re-initialised every process; \texttt{supervisor.jsonl} was append-only \emph{output}, never
read back. For a campaign specified to run for weeks across restarts this disabled every stateful
control at once: every round is ``round 1'' (five duplicate hypothesis ids were already in the
ledger), the budget can never be spent so \texttt{terminal()} never fires, dry rounds never
accumulate so \textbf{escalation was dead at the trigger}, and no proximity cluster is ever frozen.
State is now checkpointed atomically, and a corrupt checkpoint refuses to start rather than
silently resetting the counter.

\subsection{The escalation path, and its first entry}

\texttt{terminal()} has always been able to return \texttt{ESCALATE:} and nothing ever read it, so
escalation could only be reached by a crash-like condition --- never by a campaign exhausting its
reachable space, which over weeks is the normal ending. The path is now three actions tried in
order (open a stage gate; file an operator request; declare the region exhausted) and it has
produced \textbf{OR001}: quasi-static force-balance relaxation to a \emph{residual} rather than a
fixed step count, plus 3D IH/HI reconnection, with an acceptance test that today's best setting
fails by roughly $4\times$ on both integrity metrics.

\subsection{Turing $\times$ vertex: a structural limitation, measured}

The two front-page movies are the \emph{same simulation}: their archived diagnostics are identical
because \texttt{a\_sw = 50} leaves the Hill switch unfired, so the reaction--diffusion is pure
colouring. Running the loop on the settings found something sharper --- with \texttt{rho = 1.0}
every cell grows every tick, and the \texttt{conserve\_amount} dilution then drives the activator
to $2\times10^{-22}$ by frame 400. The coral pattern on the site's front page is a colour map
stretched over an extinct field.

With the field alive, 32 runs across five knobs give a clean boundary:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrrrrr}
\toprule
$\rho$ & 1.0 & 0.5 & 0.2 & 0.05 & 0.0\\
\midrule
corr(act,\,radius) & $+0.29$ & $+0.26$ & $+0.28$ & $\mathbf{+0.63}$ & $+0.53$\\
protr              & 1.03    & 1.05    & 1.06    & 1.17             & 32.7\\
hollow\_frac       & 0.000   & 0.006   & 0.008   & 0.197            & 0.839\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Eighteen runs keep an intact mesh (\texttt{hollow\_frac} $<0.05$) and reach at most
\texttt{protr} 1.064; all nine runs with \texttt{corr} $>0.4$ have \texttt{hollow\_frac}
$\geq 0.197$; \textbf{none achieves both}. On this backend activator-driven shaping and monolayer
integrity are mutually exclusive. That is a structural limitation, it is first-class, and it
reaches by measurement the conclusion the previous campaign reached independently on a different
backend: the gap is representational, not parametric.

\subsection{What the defences caught, and what they cost me}

On the first composition round the Watcher vetoed the two \emph{highest}-scoring runs ---
\texttt{protr\_peak} 4.03 and 3.20, one of them the control that three Analysts had called a tube
--- because the captions show no tube. The Critic separately refused a third slot as not-evidence
(reservoir saturated at 15\,002 cells). Two of six slots produced no evidence and both said so
loudly.

Twice in one night I proposed a mechanism and the measurement refuted it. I argued that the
per-cell volume cap explained the flat shape; the first test refuted it, but that test ran against
the extinct field, so its refutation was worthless --- \emph{a refutation obtained under a broken
instrument is not evidence, and I nearly filed it as one}. I then argued the extinction reached the
main campaign, submitted the comparison, and measured \texttt{act\_max\_final} $=0.995$ with the
dilution on: refuted within the hour. The dilution tracks the volume growth ratio, so it only bites
while cells grow; at $\rho=0$ growth saturates and the dilution stops. I had the sign of the
feedback backwards.

Both are recorded as retractions rather than quietly edited. The protocol's value is not that it
prevents wrong ideas --- it is that a wrong idea costs two cluster runs and an hour instead of a
month.
