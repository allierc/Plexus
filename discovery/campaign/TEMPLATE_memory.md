# Campaign memory

## Abstract

<THREE SENTENCES, rewritten every round. What this campaign has established, what it is doing
now, and what it is blocked on. Someone who reads only this should be able to say where the work
stands and what would change it.

It goes at the head because it is read first and most often, and because a campaign that cannot
state its position in three sentences has not understood its own position. If it takes more than
three, the surplus belongs in the sections below -- or it is not yet knowledge.>

## What is ESTABLISHED
<Claims that survived a round they could have failed in, each with the run and number that
established it, and its status. This is the knowledge the campaign has actually bought.
  - "<claim>" — SUPPORTED by <run>, <metric>=<number>, round <n>. Falsifiable by: <what would
    overturn it>.
  - "<claim>" — FALSIFIED by <run>, <metric>=<number>, round <n>. Do not re-propose.
A claim with no number is not established. Move it to OPEN.>

## What is OPEN
<Hypotheses posed and not yet settled, and WHY they are unsettled — never measured, or measured
inconclusively. An inconclusive prediction names the reason it could not be checked, because that
reason is usually a fixable defect in the prediction rather than in the biology.>

## Known traps
<Things already tried that fail, and the reason. One line each, with the run that proved it.
This section saves the most compute: an edit re-proposed is a round spent learning nothing twice.
Include APPARATUS traps the Diagnostician found, with the guard that now prevents them.>

## Frontier and parent
<What the next round breeds from, and why that and not the alternative. Name the comp hash.>

## Stability envelope
<The settings a composition must stay inside to be integrable at all, with MEASURED numbers and
the run that measured them. Not a guess.>

## Track A — the map
<What is understood about the operator landscape: which operators are necessary, sufficient,
inert. Coverage, and which cells are still blank.>

## Track B — the figure
<Which of Okuda's morphologies have been attempted and what happened. 0 of 4 is a fine answer;
"not attempted" and "attempted and failed" are different and must not be conflated.>

## Next action
<The single thing to do next, and the condition under which it changes.>
