### C5e315998af4 — driven + forced (round-33 recipe)
- **phenomenon**: A dividing, hollow cell mass extrudes a *single* long, thin, pressurized tube — one spike ~23 long and ~1.3 wide (aspect ~17.7) protruding from an otherwise hollow blastula-like body.
- **route**: Division (`divide_3d0`) builds the body up to ~5900 cells arranged around a lumen (hollow_n 1344, stable peak=final), giving a closed, pressurizable shell. The "driven+forced" recipe then adds an active internal push on top of that shell: body pressure sits at ~2.4, and where the shell first yields, material is forced out through a narrow neck. Because a narrow, tensioned tube must hold *higher* pressure than the body it drains (Laplace: p_tube 4.40 vs p_body 2.39, ratio 1.84), the first breakout self-selects — the winning tube inflates and lengthens while suppressing rivals, so exactly ONE tube forms rather than many. The `vcap=0.75` velocity cap on division throttles how fast cells are added, which keeps the extruded tube thin (diam 1.316) instead of fattening into a bud. Migration (0.144) and residual tip activity (0.27) let the tip keep advancing under the pressure gradient.
- **evidence**: ta_n_tubes_final 1; ta_tube_len_final 23.264, ta_tube_diam_final 1.316, ta_aspect_len_over_diam 17.678; mech_p_tube 4.404 > mech_p_body 2.394 (mech_p_ratio 1.839), mech_tension_mean 0.503, mech_force_mean 1.695; n_cells_final 5881; ta_hollow_n_peak = ta_hollow_n_final 1344; protr_peak 4.03 → protr_final 1.703; ta_protr_final 3.314; retention 0.423; analyst_consensus "spike" (agreement 0.67). No inert operators; not saturated; valid_evidence true. (Watcher produced no caption, so it neither gates nor blocks.)
- **forced or grown**: **Forced.** The relaxation probe is decisive: when the active drive is relaxed the protrusion collapses by 69% (Q_drop 0.69) down to Q_protr_after_relax 1.014 — i.e. near baseline. A tube *grown* by division adding structural material at the tip would persist through relaxation; this one does not. The elevated tube-over-body pressure (p_ratio 1.84) is the signature of a pressure-inflated extrusion held open by active forcing, not a self-supporting grown outgrowth. Division is still on (5881 cells) and supplies the material and the pressurizable shell, but it is not what *holds the shape*.
- **what would falsify this**: Zero out the forcing/active-drive term (the "forced" pressure source) while leaving division intact. If the route is right, the tube should collapse to the body radius (protr → baseline, p_ratio → ~1) even though cells keep dividing. Conversely, if instead the tube survives that ablation, the shape is division-grown and this account is wrong.
- **uncertainty / what would settle it**: The route is inferred from bulk mechanics + the relaxation probe, not from spatially-resolved data. What is NOT yet determined is *where* new cells go: pressure-extrusion predicts division is body-wide (tube fed by flow through the neck), whereas tip-localized division would mean the tube is partly grown despite the Q_drop. Measuring division rate along the tube axis (tip vs body) — or running the falsifier edit above — would settle forced-vs-grown directly.

### C5e315998af4 — driven + forced (round-33 recipe)
- **edit tested**: `theta divide_3d0.vcap=3.0` (companion to the `vcap=0.75` entry above; same composition, opposite end of the division velocity-cap knob).
- **phenomenon**: The same dividing, hollow body extrudes a single tube, but raising the division velocity cap 4× turns it into a much longer, thinner, far more pressurized needle — one spike ~47 long at ~1.3 wide (aspect ~35), roughly *double* the length and aspect of the vcap=0.75 variant, emerging from a more compact body with almost no lumen (hollow_n 182 vs 1344).
- **route**: `vcap` caps the velocity division may impart to daughter cells. Loosening it 4× (0.75→3.0) lets each division event push much harder, so the recipe's active drive is converted into a far stronger internal pressure source: p_body climbs to 4.65 (from 2.39) and, critically, p_tube to 21.83 (from 4.40). That higher body pressure drives the single extrusion much further out at essentially the *same* neck diameter (1.348 ≈ 1.316), so the tube gets longer and thinner-in-aspect rather than fatter. The single-tube Laplace self-selection is not just preserved but sharpened: the winning tube now sustains ~4.7× the body pressure (p_ratio 4.70 vs 1.84), a steeper pressure well that suppresses rival breakouts even more decisively — hence n_tubes still 1 with full analyst agreement ("tube", agreement 1.0, vs "spike" 0.67 before). The compact, barely-hollow body (hollow_n 182, n_cells 2448 vs 5881) is consistent with the harder, higher-velocity division channelling into a jet instead of inflating a shell around a large lumen: fewer cells accumulate and less of them line a void before the drive vents through the neck.
- **evidence**: ta_n_tubes_final 1; ta_tube_len_final 47.323, ta_tube_diam_final 1.348, ta_aspect_len_over_diam 35.106; mech_p_tube 21.826 > mech_p_body 4.646 (mech_p_ratio 4.698), mech_tension_mean 1.174, mech_force_mean 4.963; n_cells_final 2448; ta_hollow_n_peak = ta_hollow_n_final 182; protr_peak 3.22 → protr_final 2.805 (retention 0.871); ta_protr_final 5.768; mech_migration 0.144, ta_tip_act_final 0.31; analyst_consensus "tube" (agreement 1.0). No inert operators; not saturated; valid_evidence true. (Watcher produced no caption, so it neither gates nor blocks.)
- **forced or grown**: **Forced — decisively, and this run separates the two probes cleanly.** Retention 0.871 (= protr_final 2.805 / protr_peak 3.22) says the tube is *stable while the drive is on* — far steadier than the vcap=0.75 variant (0.423). But the relaxation probe removes the drive and the protrusion collapses to Q_protr_after_relax 1.014 — baseline — for an absolute Q_drop of 1.792 (~64% of protr_final). So high in-run persistence is NOT growth: the shape holds only as long as it is forced, and the very high p_ratio 4.70 is the pressure-inflation signature. Division supplies material and the pressurizable body (2448 cells) but does not hold the shape.

If the route is right, the needle collapses to body radius (protr → baseline ~1.0, p_ratio → ~1) even as cells keep dividing. If instead the tube survives, it is division-grown and this account is wrong.
- **uncertainty / what would settle it**: The vcap→pressure→length chain is inferred from the two-point vcap contrast (0.75 vs 3.0) plus bulk mechanics, not from a vcap sweep or spatial data. Two things stay open: (1) *why* length rather than diameter responds to pressure — a neck-stiffness/tension argument is plausible (tension_mean rose to 1.17) but untested; and (2) whether the reduced hollowness/cell-count is a cause of the higher pressure or a consequence of the same harder division. An intermediate vcap (e.g. 1.5) to confirm monotonic length↑/p_ratio↑, or axis-resolved division rate, would settle both.
