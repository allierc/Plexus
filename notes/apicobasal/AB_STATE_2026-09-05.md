## 2. What the change is, and why it was made

`apicobasal_geometry_3d` computed a cell's VOLUME by fanning each cap from its own centroid -- the
divergence theorem, correct -- while computing that cap's AREA as `||Newell||`, the area of the
ring's PLANAR PROJECTION. Those are two different surfaces, and the code said so in a comment: the
Newell form was chosen "so that on a right prism the two surfaces are the same arithmetic and AB-C1
is an identity rather than a near miss".

**It bought that identity with a null space.** A crumpled cap and a flat one with the same outline
have the same Newell area, and the area is stationary to first order when one vertex moves normal to
the cap -- so the surface tension does not resist crumpling at all, and only the lateral wall pushes
back. With `sep` free, the tissue relaxes downhill into a checkerboard.

MEASURED, on `gate_ab_thickshell` extended from 20 frames to 80, `sep_mu: 1.0`, and with **no**
growth, division, death or T1 in the spec:

| frame | 0 | 10 | 20 | 40 | 60 | 80 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| edge-neighbour correlation of thickness (Pearson r) | +0.12 | -0.29 | **-0.42** | -0.38 | -0.36 | -0.36 |
| median thickness `2|sep|` | 1.428 | 1.146 | 1.079 | 0.781 | 0.337 | 0.237 |
| max thickness | 1.52 | -- | -- | 6.75 | -- | 7.47 |
| inverted spans (AB-B1) | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 5 | 7 |
| AB-C4 quotient (band is 1.0 +/- 0.02) | 0.990 | -- | **0.994** | 1.113 | 1.161 | 1.152 |

Adjacent vertices anti-correlate across an edge, and at frame 80 the thickness varies five times more
WITHIN a cell's ring (cv 1.187) than BETWEEN cells (0.236): adjacent vertices of the same cap
alternate tall and short. **Quartering the step to `eta: 0.02` over `relax_iters: 120` reproduces it
and collapses slightly faster** (median 0.143 against 0.237 at frame 80), so it is this energy's own
descent direction and not a time-stepping artifact.

The fix: measure each cap on the surface its volume is measured on -- the same centroid fan, summed
as true triangle areas. No new term, no new parameter.

**The identities should survive.** For a planar convex ring fanned from its own centroid the triangle
areas sum to the polygon area exactly, so AB-C1 (flat patch, caps exactly planar) and AB-C2 (regular
hexagonal prism) are expected unchanged to the last bit. Curved shells will move, because their rings
are not planar and the cap's true area is the larger of the two -- which is exactly the case the null
space lived in. **That part is still a prediction: no gate has been re-graded.**

**THE FIX ITSELF IS VERIFIED.** `config/tissue/ab_07_section_thickshell` is the R5 spec run for 80
frames with the fixed energy, and it is the same comparison as the table above:

| | edge-neighbour correlation of thickness | thickness cv | median thickness | within-cell cv vs between-cell |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Newell cap area | +0.12 -> **-0.42** (checkerboard) | 0.021 -> **1.199** | 1.428 -> **0.237** | 1.187 vs 0.236 |
| fan cap area | +0.71 -> **+0.46** (smooth) | 0.015 -> **0.052** | 1.428 -> 0.866, levelling | **0.038 vs 0.031** |

Neighbouring vertices correlate instead of anti-correlating, the thickness field stays smooth and
nearly uniform rather than bifurcating, and the median settles near 0.87 instead of collapsing toward
zero. The checkerboard is gone. What remains visible in the render at frame 80 is the shell's own
buckling, which is a shape and not a crumple.

## 3. What this does to R5, which was reported green

`gate_ab_thickshell` is graded at frame 20. The table above shows the instability is already at
r = -0.42 there and still growing; AB-C4 reads 0.9936 at frame 20 and 1.15 by frame 80. **R5's rows
are green on a state that had not settled, and the run was not checked for settling.** The agreed
repair is to re-run and re-grade after the fix, extending the gate past frame 20 so the graded state
is a settled one rather than an unsettled one that happens to pass.

R3 and R4 are unaffected in principle -- they run at `sep_mu: 0`, where `sep` cannot move -- but R4's
shell is curved, so its trajectory moves with the cap-area change even though its rows are about
volume. Both still need re-running.

## 4. R6, where the work stopped

`config/gates/gate_ab_population.yaml` is WRITTEN AND HAS NEVER BEEN GRADED. Ten rows: AB-B4
(euler), AB-B3, AB-B10 (scutoids), AB-B5 (occ), AB-B6 (renumber), the span-zero carry row, AB-B9,
AB-B1, a deaths-happened row and AB-M4 (doubling time). Four new measures back it, all in
`tools/gate_measures.py` and all registered: `cell_face_count_residual`, `scutoid_fraction`,
`apicobasal_span_zero_fraction` and (from R4/R5) `shell_asphericity`, `basal_cap_collapse_fraction`,
`cell_thickness_fold`.

What the R6 pilots established before the fix, worth keeping:

* **At 200 cells with `h0: 0.4` nothing divides in 80 frames.** `cell_divide` fires on
  `v >= factor * jit * v_ref` with `v_ref` the seed-time median, so a cell must reach 2x the seed
  volume and `cell_grow.rate: 0.003457` needs ~200 frames to get there. The reference gate runs 401
  frames for this reason.
* **At 1280 cells with `h0: 1.8` division works** -- 1280 -> 4117 cells in 80 frames -- and the
  doubling time came out at 7.9 h against AB-M4's inherited band of 12-24 h. That number was measured
  on the crumpling energy and means nothing until it is re-run.
* `cell_die[crowded]` with `n_max: 8` was chosen so death fires from the tissue's own state rather
  than from a hand-written list. **It completed no extrusions in 80 frames** (`n_apop` stayed 0),
  so either the run must be long enough for `edge_flip` to shed a marked cell down to a triangle, or
  `n_max` must come down. Untested either way.
