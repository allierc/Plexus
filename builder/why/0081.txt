R5's beat is real and periodic but it happens in a vacuum; this asks the only question that matters for a swimmer -- does the stroke move anything that is not the animal. 98,808 animal points and 140,000 seawater points scatter into ONE mpm_grid, which is the whole coupling: mpm_scatter zeroes the grid only on the substep's first scatter and accumulates thereafter, so the two sets exert forces on each other through the grid's momentum. Nothing applies a force to the water. The water is a liquid and says so -- bulk_modulus instead of youngs, plus mpm_viscosity, because without the viscosity a liquid here is a pressure-only gas that shears not at all, and the wake this rung exists to see is a shear wake.
