why    SHEAR-WAVE BENCH (nu_E_g32_eta1): a box of water, a shear wave u = 0.5 sin(2 pi z) x_hat, and nothing else. It decays as exp(-nu k^2 t), so fitting the amplitude returns the EFFECTIVE kinematic viscosity -- the physical one PLUS whatever the discretisation adds. This matters because every Reynolds number I have quoted in this model came from the spec's eta, and in MLS-MPM the particle-to-grid-to-particle transfer smooths the velocity every substep: that numerical viscosity is about dx^2/(2 dt) or U dx / 2, which on the cilium runs was 0.244 against a physical 1e-6. If so, the GRID set the dissipation, the spec's eta did nothing, and those runs sat near Re 4 and not the 9e5 I claimed. The design is a 2x2 in (dx, dt) plus one: the two candidate laws differ in whether dt matters -- dx^2/(2 dt) does, U dx / 2 does not -- so grid and substep are varied INDEPENDENTLY, and a fifth run raises eta to 1.0, far above either estimate, to check the physical viscosity takes control once it is no longer buried. Particles per cell is held at 8.00 throughout, because varying the grid at a fixed particle COUNT would change the sampling and measure that instead. The wave varies in z and points in x, which makes it pure SHEAR; pointed along the axis it varies in it would be a sound wave and measure nothing about viscosity. Walls are frictionless and perfectly elastic, since a damping wall removes energy of its own and would be counted as viscosity. (before the run)
